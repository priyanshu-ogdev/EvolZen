    Phase 1.4 — Directed Exploration & Verified Hypothesis Formation

    Subsystem: evolzen.exploration Code: src/evolzen/exploration/

    Goal

    Use an actor/synthesizer/verifier loop to propose typed rules and capability packages for the persistent world. Exploration is prioritized by a measured explanation gap over agent types, mechanics, and world-growth categories. An off-the-shelf coding-agent harness may serve as the synthesizer, but generated code is untrusted and must execute only inside evolzen.containment.Sandbox.

    Exact replay is necessary but not sufficient. Promotion requires replay on held-out transition combinations, at least one controlled intervention, declared resource bounds, and an explicit validity context.

    What you build

    • actor probes and ontology/explanation-gap prioritization;
    • typed hypothesis and capability-package schemas;
    • sandboxed synthesis and execution;
    • replay, held-out-combination, intervention, and resource-bound verification; and
    • false-promotion, false-rejection, and exploration-cost measurements.

    Success metric

    Verified hypotheses beat a no-exploration-strategy baseline on held-out transitions and controlled interventions, while false promotions remain below a pre-registered threshold and no hypothesis can mutate the engine outside the versioned capability boundary.

    Citations

    • Peer-reviewed journals / conferences: Romera-Paredes et al. (FunSearch, Nature 2024, DOI:10.1038/s41586-023-06924-6) for evaluator-gated LLM evolutionary program search; Pathak et al. (ICML 2017, arXiv:1705.05363) for self-supervised prediction-error curiosity; Wang et al. (Voyager, TMLR 2023) for self-verifying code synthesis.
    • Preprints: OPINE-World (arXiv:2607.01531), Tycho (arXiv:2607.28287), and Executable World Models for ARC-AGI-3 (arXiv:2605.05138).

    Benchmark results in isolated games or math problems do not establish transfer to a persistent social sandbox; that transfer is an explicit EvolZen experiment and is untested.

    Research context & breakthroughs

    • Verifier-gated program search: FunSearch (Nature, 2024) and Executable World Models (arXiv:2605.05138) showed that pairing LLM code generators with deterministic evaluators discovers novel verifiable programs while avoiding ungrounded hallucinations.
    • Ontology-error & curiosity prioritization: Pathak et al. (ICML 2017) introduced forward-model prediction error as an intrinsic exploration signal, while OPINE-World (arXiv:2607.01531) and Tycho (arXiv:2607.28287) prioritize probing actions that falsify current programmatic world hypotheses.

    Codebase progress match

    • Upstream Phase 1.1 contracts implemented: SocialSandboxEngine, replay_transitions, apply_intervention, TransitionLogStore, and CapabilityPackage (with evidence_id, verified_replay, verified_intervention, verified_containment) are implemented in evolzen.environment, evolzen.schemas.world, and evolzen.storage.
    • Actor (src/evolzen/exploration/actor.py): Selects probes by ontology/explanation gap (1 test in tests/exploration/test_actor.py).
    • Synthesizer (src/evolzen/exploration/synthesizer.py): CandidateRule, Synthesizer protocol, and TemplateRuleSynthesizer generating executable Python prediction functions from observed transitions (4 tests in tests/exploration/test_synthesizer.py).
    • Verifier (src/evolzen/exploration/verifier.py): Implemented Verifier with VerificationReport executing rule hypotheses exclusively inside evolzen.containment.Sandbox. Supports exact-match replay across held-out transitions, counterfactual intervention verification (verify_intervention), and capability promotion (promote_to_capability_package) accepted by CapabilityGovernor and SocialSandboxEngine (9 tests in tests/exploration/test_verifier.py).
    • Total test coverage: 14 tests passing across tests/exploration/.
    • Open cross-phase alignment items:
    • Multi-generational autonomous coding-agent LLM harness benchmarks on hardware rig. Empirical exploration transfer remains untested.
