    Phase 1.2 — The Agents

    Subsystem: evolzen.agents Code: src/evolzen/agents/

    Detailed design: developmental agents. Implemented increment: agents/development/ and schemas/development.py contain a numerical lifecycle/learning/teaching/archive baseline. LLM training and full population/world integration remain pending.

    Goal

    Populate the persistent world with 10–30 active cognitive agents plus optional deterministic background population processes. Each active agent has an explicit behavioral/social profile: needs, drives, skills, roles, affiliations, memory scope, communication channels, norms, capabilities, and lineage. “All types of people” is operationalized as coverage of this ontology, not as a claim to reproduce real human demographics or psychology.

    Drive vectors are evolved on the vector itself, never by literal crossover of independently trained neural weights. Quality-Diversity selection preserves declared behavioral and social descriptors. Self-modification is optional in the first baseline and, when enabled, is sandboxed and accepted only after held-out improvement plus invariant checks.

    What you build

    • exactly eight birth-randomized motivational coordinates: pride, greed, lust, envy, gluttony, wrath, sloth, Null; a safe bounded equation family rather than generated source programs;
    • child/adolescent/adult/elder/dead stages on a world clock, with competence measured separately;
    • personal outcome learning and living-parent advice, without copying mastery at birth;

    • bounded numeric temperament inheritance and validation-gated behavioral archive admission;

    • an AgentProfile schema and an explicit descriptor map;

    • population and lineage snapshots;
    • QD archive coverage, occupancy, persistence, extinction, replacement, specialization, and communication metrics;
    • a sparse communication/trade graph and non-authoritative role structure; and
    • a hardware pilot that records model memory, token throughput, action latency, and scheduling overhead on the actual rig.

    Success metric

    First satisfy developmental primitive gates: reproducible equations, action/feedback adaptation, life boundaries, parent teaching, and archive safety. Then measure cultural transfer, age-versus-experience and eight-axis controls on held-out worlds/families. Passing unit tests does not satisfy the population success metric below.

    A 10–30-agent population runs stably for at least five generations, maintains measurable coverage across the declared descriptor map, and produces at least two persistent role/specialization patterns without collapsing to one strategy. Any self-modification improvement is reported separately from population diversity.

    Citations

    • Peer-reviewed IEEE / journal foundations: Oudeyer, Kaplan & Hafner (2007, IEEE TEVC, DOI:10.1109/TEVC.2006.890271) for intrinsic motivation in developmental learning; Merrick (2010, IEEE TAMD/TCDS, DOI:10.1109/TAMD.2010.2051435) for comparative intrinsic value-system benchmarking; Santucci, Baldassarre & Mirolli (2016, IEEE TCDS, DOI:10.1109/TCDS.2016.2538961) for goal-discovering developmental architectures; Vassiliades, Chatzilygeroudis & Mouret (2018, IEEE TEVC, DOI:10.1109/TEVC.2017.2735550) for CVT-MAP-Elites scaling to higher-dimensional behavioral descriptors; Pugh, Soros & Stanley (2016, Frontiers in Robotics and AI, DOI:10.3389/frobt.2016.00040) for separating quality from behavioral diversity; Hayes et al. (2022, JAAMAS, DOI:10.1007/s10458-022-09552-y) for MORL linear vs. non-linear Chebyshev scalarization; Keramati & Gutkin (2014, eLife, DOI:10.7554/eLife.04811) for homeostatic drive-reduction utility; Colas et al. (2022, JAIR, arXiv:2012.09830) for autotelic goal-conditioned agents; Asadi & Littman (2017, ICML, arXiv:1612.05628) for Mellowmax softmax stability.
    • Conference / preprints: Darwin Gödel Machine (arXiv:2505.22954, ICLR 2026), Hyperagents (arXiv:2603.19461, preprint), Quality-Diversity with Limited Resources (Wang et al., ICML 2024, arXiv:2406.03731), Cook et al. (Artificial Generational Intelligence, arXiv:2406.00392, 2024), and Generative Agents (Park et al., ACM UIST 2023 / arXiv:2304.03442). Continual experience replay (Rolnick et al., CLEAR, NeurIPS 2019, arXiv:1811.11682) governs Phase 2.1 rehearsal replay, while Phase 1.2 runtime employs verified contextual EMA outcome estimation and intergenerational manual teaching (teach_manual).

    The eight-drive ontology, descriptor map, and transfer from these precedents to a persistent social sandbox remain untested; cited diversity-collapse studies are treated as hypotheses.

    Research context & breakthroughs

    • Intrinsic motivation & developmental stages: IEEE TEVC (Oudeyer et al., 2007) and IEEE TCDS (Santucci et al., 2016) formalize competence-progress and autonomous goal discovery across developmental lifetimes—highlighting that EvolZen's current greedy candidate ranking (decide) is a numerical baseline requiring a controlled exploration comparison.
    • Quality-Diversity scaling: MAP-Elites and CVT-MAP-Elites (IEEE TEVC, 2018; Pugh et al., 2016; Wang et al., 2024) replace single-objective fitness selection with behavioral repertoire maintenance, directly countering LLM multi-agent homogenization (ACL 2026 Findings diversitycollapse2026).
    • Self-modification & metacognitive risk: DGM (ICLR 2026) and Hyperagents (arXiv:2603.19461) show empirical archive-gated self-editing and emergent persistent memory structures, underscoring why self-modification in EvolZen is gated by DevelopmentArchive and evolzen.containment.Sandbox.

    Codebase progress match

    • Implemented unified developmental agent architecture (src/evolzen/agents/, src/evolzen/schemas/):
    • Identity & profile: AgentProfile (schemas/agent_profile.py), replacing the legacy five-axis DriveVector with rich social, institutional, norm, and behavioral descriptor tracking.
    • Developmental runtime: Agent (agents/agent.py), unifying AgentProfile, AgentDevelopmentState, lifecycle staging (stage_at), and host-adapter integration.
    • Decision policy & exploration: Augmented Chebyshev scalarization (brain.py), UCB1 epistemic exploration bonus, and Mellowmax temperature sampling.
    • Structured LLM inference: InferenceBackend & TransformersBackend (agents/inference.py), providing outlines-constrained schema-valid action generation with deterministic safe fallback.
    • Scaled quality-diversity archive: CVTArchive & QualityDiversityArchive (agents/qd_archive.py), implementing Centroidal Voronoi Tessellations across $k$-D behavioral descriptors (vassiliades2018cvtmape).
    • Sandboxed self-modification: SelfModificationProposal & evaluate_proposal (agents/self_modification.py), enforcing default-deny execution inside evolzen.containment.Sandbox.
    • Population scheduler & teaching: PopulationScheduler (agents/scheduler.py), orchestrating generational rollouts, living-parent skill advice, recipe manual transmission (teach_manual), and DuckDB transition persistence.
    • Comprehensive test coverage: Covered by 58 passing test cases across tests/agents/ (including test_agent.py, test_inference.py, test_manual_teaching.py, test_qd_archive.py, test_self_modification.py, test_population_integration.py, and tests/agents/development/).
    • Implemented Phase 1.1 environment host bridge (src/evolzen/environment/adapter.py, engine.py, pettingzoo_env.py): SocialSandboxEngine, WorldState/ActionType (schemas/world.py), SocialSandboxAECEnv, and adapter (observe_features, legal_candidates, action_from_candidate, execute_decision).
    • Retired legacy path: Legacy 5-axis DriveVector (schemas/drive_vector.py, agents/drive_vector.py) cleanly retired per Option A architectural consensus.
    • Open alignment & empirical items:
    • Multi-generation empirical benchmarking runs on hardware pilot;
    • GPU tensor-parallel live serving via VLLMBackend;
    • Expanding adapter.legal_candidates from 7 to all 17 actions in SocialSandboxEngine.
