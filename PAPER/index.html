    Paper Outline

    A skeleton for the research paper this project is meant to produce, mapping each section onto material that already exists elsewhere in this repository. Fill in results as they're measured; don't fill in claims ahead of measurement — every placeholder below says exactly what evidence it's waiting on.

    Working title

    EvolZen: Measuring Persistent World Growth and Fresh-World Adaptation in a Grounded, Evolutionarily-Diverse Multi-Agent Sandbox

    (Or shorter: EvolZen: A Testbed for Generalization Efficiency in Grounded Multi-Agent Systems.)

    Abstract

    Draft once Phase 2.4/2.5 results exist. One paragraph: the two questions (does the world grow durably under endogenous processes, and does the learning system improve fresh-world adaptation?), the method (nine subsystems, briefly), the persistent-world and EAE results, the ablation outcome, and the honest scope (bounded evidence about a defined sandbox — not a claim about general intelligence, emotional intelligence, or real human psychology).

    1. Introduction

    • The difference between plausible narrative behavior and replayable, persistent world dynamics (docs/PRD.md §2, docs/research/design-audit.md).
    • The two research questions this project tests (docs/PRD.md §1).
    • Contribution statement: draft from docs/PRD.md §1's summary sentence directly — resist the temptation to strengthen it once results are in hand; if results support a stronger statement, that's a finding to report, not a reason to have written it earlier.

    2. Related Work

    Pull directly from docs/research/literature-review.md and docs/research/landscape.md. Six threads, each with its own subsection: World Models, Continual Learning, Verified World-Model Synthesis, Program-Synthesis Memory, Population Diversity, Data Provenance. State which techniques are hypotheses, which are precedents, and how source status and exposure history constrain the paper's evaluation design.

    3. Method / Architecture

    One subsection per subsystem, drawn from docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §3: environment, agents, economy, exploration, library, predictor, containment, recognition, evaluation. Each subsection: mechanism, why it was chosen over the alternative the original design used, citation. This is close to a direct copy of docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §3, trimmed of implementation-file references that don't belong in a paper (e.g., drop moe_library.py-style pointers, keep the mechanism descriptions).

    4. Design Evolution (optional section, or fold into Related Work / Appendix)

    docs/research/objectives-and-evaluation.md is the authority for research questions, hypotheses, baselines, split rules, and decision gates. docs/research/design-evolution.md in full is likely too long for the main paper body, but its existence and structure (every mechanism traced from its original, unsupported version to a cited, buildable one) is itself worth a paragraph — reviewers evaluating a design this iterative benefit from seeing that the iteration was principled, not ad hoc. Consider an appendix with the full table, and a one-paragraph summary in the main text pointing to it.

    5. Evaluation

    • Setup: the persistent-world generator and fresh-world generator (evolzen.evaluation.procedural_env_gen), with exposure, manual, replay, and seed history recorded for every run.
    • Persistent-world metrics: durable growth, world health, population/type diversity, lineage persistence, shock recovery, and extinction/replacement (docs/PRD.md §9).
    • Learning metrics: forgetting, forward/backward transfer, adaptation latency, and update cost.
    • Ablation matrix: with/without recognition model x with/without manual layer x JEPA-only / wake-sleep-only / combined (docs/phases/2.3-recognition.md). Report all cells, not just the best one.
    • Fresh-world result: EAE on genuinely unseen seeds/mechanic combinations. Use human or frontier reference points only when observations, action space, and time budget match; do not inherit ARC-AGI-3 numbers as if they were directly comparable.
    • Exposure result: reused-vs-fresh performance and all manual/replay access history for this system specifically (evolzen.evaluation.report.EvaluationReport makes this required).

    6. Results

    Empty until measured. Structure the table to match EvaluationReport's fields exactly, so writing this section is substituting numbers into a pre-agreed shape rather than deciding what to report after seeing what looks good.

    7. Limitations & Honest Positioning

    Draft directly from docs/PRD.md §4 (Non-Goals) and docs/research/design-evolution.md's closing section ("Why the ceiling holds regardless of any upgrade above"):

    • Generality is bounded by task and world diversity — a result within this environment family is not evidence about real human psychology or general intelligence.
    • No component here distills past its teacher without an external reward; everything is bounded by the base models plus whatever real signal evolzen.environment provides.
    • If programmatic verification, the recognition model, or the proposed world-growth mechanisms fail on the persistent social sandbox, that is reported here as a finding, not omitted.

    8. Conclusion

    One paragraph, restating the defensible claim from docs/PRD.md §1 with the actual measured result slotted in, and one sentence on what a negative result would still contribute to the field (a citable answer that this specific combination doesn't transfer, ruling out a real hypothesis other groups might otherwise spend time on).

    References

    Generate from references.bib directly — every citation used in this outline already has an entry there.

    A note on scope creep, for whoever drafts this

    The single most common way a paper drawn from this project could go wrong is tightening the Abstract or Conclusion's language beyond what the persistent-world and fresh-world results support — see AGENTS.md's "Safety-critical constraints" #4 and docs/PRD.md §4 (NG1). If a draft sentence would read differently to a skeptical reviewer than to the person who ran the experiment, that's the sentence to rewrite before submission, not after review.
