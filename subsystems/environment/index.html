    Environment integration

    Authority: Phase 1.1. Source package: src/evolzen/environment/.

    Own the world clock, agent observations, legal candidate generation, execution and outcome verification. Developmental agents consume eight normalized observable features and return a pending action ID; they do not mutate world state themselves. The environment must recheck age, resources, permissions and action parameters, then issue a matching trusted outcome receipt. Version context/skill keys with mechanics and meaningful action parameters.

    Implemented Phase 1.1 core: - SocialSandboxEngine (engine.py) implementing Environment and PersistentEnvironment over (state_t, action_t, exogenous_input_t, seed) -> (emission_t, state_{t+1}), covering all 17 ActionTypes with verified CapabilityPackage gating (no arbitrary engine mutation); - canonical WorldState, WorldSnapshot, CapabilityPackage, GrowthEvent, WorldInvariant, InvariantReport, BranchRecord, and InterventionSpec contracts (evolzen.schemas.world); - check_invariants (invariants.py), extract_growth_events / summarize_growth (growth.py) across all five GrowthCategory buckets; - TransitionLogStore (evolzen.storage.transition_log, SCHEMA_VERSION = 1) for JSON-only DuckDB persistence, SHA-256 hash-chained transition appends, snapshots, growth events, and atomic save_snapshot_and_branch; - replay_transitions, replay_from_store, and branch_from_snapshot (replay.py); - apply_intervention (interventions.py); - adapter (observe_features, legal_candidates, action_from_candidate, execute_decision) for evolzen.agents.development, including independent host-side verification of Decision legality and minimum_age; - pettingzoo_env (SocialSandboxAECEnv) wrapping SocialSandboxEngine under PettingZoo's AECEnv contract (Discrete(10) with action_mask or typed Action); - run_environment_pilot (pilot.py) for local CPU throughput/storage benchmarking with snapshot_interval support.

    Landed Stage 1 critical fixes: - replay_transitions passes current_state (not recorded.state) to engine.step, propagating state divergence accurately; - replay_from_store includes transitions up to up_to_tick, preserving same_tick=True multi-agent transitions; - apply_intervention supports sequence indexing (_set_nested_path) and reconciles ResourceAccounting.initial_currency; - _handle_spawn_offspring and _check_bounded_state evaluate capacity against active living agents rather than historical lineage records.

    Landed Stage 2 host adapter full 17-action coverage: - adapter.legal_candidates and adapter.action_from_candidate cover all 17 ActionTypes (wait, gather, move, consume, craft, trade, propose_contract, fulfill_contract, found_institution, join_institution, contribute_institution, propose_norm, adopt_norm, assign_role, spawn_offspring, improve_location, register_capability); - Added cancel_contract:{cid} candidate generation and parsing for proposers of open contracts; - Host-side validation in execute_decision verifies legal candidate membership, dynamic permissions, and minimum age before stepping and returning verified Outcome receipts.

    Landed Stage 3 operational mechanics: - Tool Durability & Fatigue Mitigation: Handlers _handle_gather and _handle_improve_location consume tool durability on work actions while halving physical fatigue accumulation; - Norm Compliance & Social Friction: _handle_gather evaluates conservation norms during common-pool harvesting, penalizing hypocrisy (-0.1 status/social reward) and community friction (-0.05 status/social reward) during resource scarcity (< 20% cap) while rewarding sustainable harvests (+0.02); - Institutional Trade Fee Withholding & Reserves: _handle_trade withholds contribution_rate fee on local trades directly to local_inst.currency_reserve while maintaining exact resource_conservation invariant; _handle_contribute_institution supports direct currency contributions; - Contract Cancellation & Expiration Sweep: Handlers support voluntary cancellation (cancel=True in _handle_fulfill_contract) and automatic expiration sweep (_expire_contracts) for stale open contracts exceeding TTL (100 ticks) or with deceased parties; - FR-2 Bounded Collections & Status: Enforced upper bounds on WorldAgentState.status ([0.0, 1.0]) across schemas, invariants, and engine handlers. Enforced capacity bounds (artifacts <= 512, contracts <= 256, institutions <= 64, norms <= 64, capabilities <= 64) in invariants.py and action rejection; - Exogenous Provenance: _apply_exogenous captures unhandled exogenous events (unhandled:<kind>) in applied_exog and records them in transition emissions (exogenous_applied).

    Landed Stage 4 PettingZoo AECEnv upgrades & endogenous process attribution: - PettingZoo Macro-Shocks & Seeding: SocialSandboxAECEnv.step() accepts exogenous_input and deterministic seed kwargs, allowing RL and AEC callers to simulate macroeconomic shocks and seed schedules; - TransitionLogStore Hook: SocialSandboxAECEnv.__init__ accepts optional store: TransitionLogStore | None = None and automatically appends every executed transition with DuckDB hash-chain linkage; surfaces env.last_transition and env.store; - Expanded 16-Action Discrete Space: Expanded _act_space to Discrete(16) with full typed translation and action masks for trade, institution joining, contributions, norm proposals, norm adoptions, and reproduction alongside physical foraging/crafting; - Endogenous Process Growth Attribution: Added _expand_endogenous_ecology in engine.py, passively expanding carrying capacity by 1 for undisturbed flourishing ecosystems (shock_factor == 1.0, full resource capacity, next_tick % 50 == 0), activating the previously dormant GrowthAttribution.ENDOGENOUS_PROCESS emitter in growth.py.

    Open Phase 1.1 hardening backlog (empirical metrics remain untested / unmeasured): - Untested validation & downstream scope: all 18 failure branches in invariants.py, Hypothesis property-based tests, empirical long-horizon Phase 1.1 runs, SocialSandboxAECEnv multi-agent learning integrations, LLM population scheduling (evolzen.agents, Phase 1.2), full market/manual provenance flows (evolzen.economy, Phase 1.3), sandboxed capability synthesis (evolzen.exploration, evolzen.containment, Phases 1.4/2.2), and target-rig hardware pilot measurements on DGX/AGX Spark. See agent engineering for the broader boundaries.
