    Motivation: a randomized, bounded birth equation

    Untested design hypothesis. Coordinate semantics and equations below are invented for EvolZen. They must be compared with simpler controls, not treated as laws of personality.

    Eight coordinates

    All vectors use this order, recorded by Motive in schemas/development.py.

    Coordinate Observable incentive Distinction and test
    Pride Reputation, status, mastery recognition Choose public credit versus identical private reward
    Greed Retained assets and future control of resources Save versus consume under equal survival need
    Lust Nonsexual attraction, affiliation and preferred experiences Social contact versus solitude; no sexualized child content
    Envy Relative disadvantage against a visible comparison group Hold own assets fixed while changing observed peer assets
    Gluttony Immediate consumption/satiation beyond baseline need Consume now versus retain resources; not identical to greed
    Wrath Response to perceived obstruction, threat or unfairness Protest/defend/repair under permitted action constraints
    Sloth Avoidance of effort and resource expenditure Lower-cost method of achieving the same end
    Null Disengagement/absence of goal pursuit Wait/abstain even where another zero-effort action exists

    Low wrath does not mechanically imply kindness; high pride may encourage mastery or bluffing depending on opportunities and learned beliefs. Cooperation and care can emerge from multiple combinations but are not guaranteed or adequately modeled by these eight dimensions. Do not equate demographic identity with coefficient values. Future extensions need their own ablations.

    Inputs and normalization

    The environment adapter produces an eight-element feature vector x_t in [-1, 1] with versioned feature definitions: status discrepancy, resource discrepancy, affiliation opportunity, relative disadvantage, consumption pressure, obstruction, fatigue, and disengagement opportunity. Each feature must come from the agent's permitted observations/internal needs, not evaluator secrets. Values outside the range, NaN, infinity and wrong dimensions are rejected, not clipped silently. Feature scaling is fixed on training data and logged with the scenario version.

    For agent i, drive k at decision n:

    a_k(n) = tanh(b_k + (1/8) sum_j W_kj x_j(n)
              + c_k age_fraction(n) + rho_k a_k(n-1))
p_k(n) = exp((a_k(n) - max(a(n))) / T) / sum_l exp((a_l(n) - max(a(n))) / T)

    age_fraction = (world_tick - born_at) / lifespan for living agents. All eight ratios are nonnegative and sum to one; activations are bounded [-1, 1]. A ratio is relative priority, not a probability that the agent will commit a particular act. Null is an explicit competing coordinate; it is not 1 - sum(other unnormalized values).

    Founder coefficients are sampled with a local seeded generator: b,c uniform [-0.5,0.5], W uniform [-1,1], rho uniform [0,0.5], T=1. Coefficients are frozen birth data. Seeds and full coefficients are persisted; seed alone is not a portable serialization format. Inheritance averages aligned numerical coefficients plus bounded uniform mutation; rho stays [0,1]. T remains the parent mean. No equations are strings, and no eval or generated Python is used.

    The equation advances once per decision, not every empty world tick. This is a simple recurrence, not a continuous-time ODE. Compare decision-rate-matched agents to avoid confounding momentum with action frequency. Periodicity, cross-drive recurrence, homeostatic feedback and learned equations are future variants, not features of v1.

    From preferences to choices

    Each domain candidate has an immutable action ID, context key, skill key, eight predicted outcome values, cost, minimum age and domain permission flag. Context/skill keys must distinguish materially different action parameters and world-rule versions.

    score(action) = sum_k p_k * expected_outcome_k(action) - cost(action)

    Expected outcomes are the agent's own empirical estimate when available. Otherwise they are the supplied candidate prediction, blended with at most 25% parental advice (10% default). Own experience overrides parental advice. The highest score wins; action ID breaks ties, making candidate ordering irrelevant. Candidate counts are bounded. If no candidate is legal the call fails; the host must supply an explicit legal wait action when needed.

    Theoretical grounding & formal classification

    While the seven-sins-plus-Null names and $8 \times 8$ tendency matrix $W_{kj}$ are untested EvolZen design choices, their mathematical form maps directly onto three established frameworks in IEEE and ML literature:

    1. Homeostatic Drive Modulation (Keramati & Gutkin, eLife 2014, keramati2014homeostatic): The context vector $x_t \in [-1, 1]^8$ encodes discrepancies (resource deficit, status gap, consumption pressure, fatigue). Modulating drive activations $a_k(n)$ from internal and environmental deviations functions as a bounded homeostatic weighting mechanism rather than a claim about human emotion.
    2. Multi-Objective Linear Scalarization (Hayes et al., AAMAS 2022, hayes2022morl): Evaluating score(action) = sum_k p_k * expected_outcome_k(action) - cost(action) is mathematically MORL linear scalarization using dynamic preference weights $p_k(n)$ over an 8-dimensional reward vector. By MORL theory, linear scalarization can only recover policies on the convex hull of the Pareto front; whenever trade-offs (e.g., safety vs. immediate consumption) are non-convex, future variants must ablate non-linear (Chebyshev) utility instead of assuming linear weights suffice.
    3. Priority Ratios vs. Stochastic Policy Operators (Asadi & Littman, ICML 2017, asadi2017mellowmax): drive_step uses Boltzmann softmax solely to normalize $a_k(n)$ into positive priority weights $p_k(n)$, while decide uses deterministic argmax (ranked[0]). If future versions convert action scores into stochastic exploration policies, standard Boltzmann softmax can suffer from non-contraction/multiple fixed points under value iteration; the Mellowmax operator provides a provably non-expansive alternative to test against greedy selection and $\epsilon$-greedy.

    This baseline is greedy and does not implement curiosity, planning or autonomous exploration (compare Merrick, IEEE TAMD 2010, merrick2010valuesystems; Colas et al., JAIR 2022, colas2022autotelic). A controlled exploration schedule, intrinsic competence-progress term, or LLM proposal distribution must be added as a separately measured condition. Predicted utility is not factual truth; observed outcome updates are how the baseline corrects a mistaken preference-to-action expectation.

    Preferences are not permissions

    Candidate masks are only an initial host-provided filter. The environment independently checks action legality and lifecycle permissions on execution. A high wrath or greed ratio cannot grant network access, change the reward evaluator, override containment, or alter another agent's memory. Hard constraints sit outside utility maximization; violations cannot be traded for reward.

    For future LLM reasoning, use ratios as structured goal-selection and memory-retrieval inputs, then compare against ranking-only. Do not claim that a prompt changes internal neural reasoning without controlled evidence. Log concise goal IDs, candidates, scores and evidence—not hidden chain-of-thought.
