    Strategic Blueprint: Next Research & Engineering Frontiers for EvolZen

    Document Status: Phase 0 Complete / Post-Horizon 4 Strategic Blueprint
    Target Systems: CPU Local Simulation, Ray Distributed Cluster, NVIDIA DGX Spark, Jetson AGX Spark, RTX A6000
    Authors: EvolZen Swarm Intelligence Team (strategic-planner)
    Date: November 2026

    1. Executive Summary & Verified Baseline State

    With the sequential execution and rigorous verification of Options 1–4 and Horizons 1–4, the EvolZen research testbed has achieved a battle-hardened foundation:

    1. Subsystem Verification: 392 passing tests (1 skipped on Darwin host due to Linux-specific runsc gVisor sandboxing requirement), 0 mypy strict type errors across 144 source and test modules, clean ruff linting, and strict MkDocs documentation build.
    2. Empirical Telemetry: 1,576 transition steps logged with cryptographic SHA-256 hash-chain integrity in data/transitions.duckdb, proving generational progression to generation 16 (empirically exceeding the Phase 1.2 $\ge 5$ generation milestone), 63 births, 65 deaths, 1,952 teaching events, and multi-niche CVTArchive coverage.
    3. Operational Tooling: 7 operational CLI entrypoints (run, train, evaluate, replay, inspect, ablate, cluster), complete with an interactive HTML5/Canvas visualization dashboard (dashboard.html) and an automated multi-node pre-flight hardware diagnostic harness.

    This blueprint articulates the next major research and engineering frontiers, categorizing immediate implementation steps, empirical campaigns, and physical GPU rig deployments into actionable tiers.

    2. Deep Dive: The Four Next Major Frontiers

    Frontier 1: Longitudinal Cultural Evolution & Multi-Lineage Market Dynamics

    Motivation & Research Questions

    Current simulations validate multi-agent generational lifecycle transitions over short runs (5 generations, 1,576 ticks). However, biological and cultural evolutionary dynamics exhibit critical phase transitions, demographic bottlenecks, and institutional drift only over long-horizon evolutionary time: 1. Demographic Replacement & Senescent Stability: Under a developmental lifecycle ($A_{\text{adol}}=15, A_{\text{adult}}=25, A_{\text{elder}}=200, A_{\text{life}}=350$), does the population achieve steady-state birth-death equilibrium over 50–100 generations (10,000–50,000 world ticks), or does it succumb to extinction bottlenecks caused by senescence waves? 2. Norm Diffusion & Provenance Integrity: With real_data_floor=0.2 in ManualStore, do human-authored foundational recipes (ProvenanceCategory.HUMAN_AUTHORED) persist against synthetic degradation and mutation over 50+ generations, or does cultural transmission suffer catastrophic noise accumulation (Henrich & Boyd 2002; Henrich & McElreath 2003)? 3. Wealth Inequality & Gini Resilience Under Exogenous Resource Shocks: When the environment experiences exogenous resource scarcity shocks (periodic regenerative droughts), does the decentralized double-auction economy buffer agents against starvation, or does it trigger wealth hyper-concentration ($\text{Gini} > 0.65$)?

    Research Hypotheses & Target Metrics

    • Hypothesis 1.1 (Macro-Demographic Equilibrium): A 10–30 agent population seeded with diverse initial genomes (sample_genome) maintains non-zero living population and active lineages across 100 generations without external intervention.
    • Hypothesis 1.2 (Cultural Preservation Threshold): With vertical and oblique teaching active, human-authored crafting recipes maintain $\ge 80\%$ adoption fidelity across 50 generations when real_data_floor \ge 0.2.
    • Hypothesis 1.3 (Market Inequality Damping): Double-auction exchange with perishable goods dampens wealth Gini volatility by $\ge 25\%$ during drought shocks compared to an autarkic barter baseline.

    Frontier 2: Model-Based Policy Rollouts & Offline Planning (evolzen train)

    Motivation & Research Questions

    Currently, agents in SocialSandboxEngine select actions primarily through reflexive 8-drive satisfaction heuristics. evolzen train now jointly trains the WorldDynamicsPredictor, the GroundedJEPAEncoder, and the Planner using GRPO (Generalized Reinforcement Policy Optimization; Shao et al., 2024) and CLEAR rehearsal buffering (Rolnick et al., 2019). The next milestone is closing the loop: 1. Model-Based vs. Reflexive Heuristics: Do inference-time tree rollouts over the learned world model (WorldDynamicsPredictor) outperform reflexive heuristics on held-out procedural tasks in DualTrackRunner (Track 2 EAE_B action efficiency and solve rate)? 2. Compounding Prediction Errors vs. Planning Horizon: At what planning depth $H \in {1, 3, 5, 10}$ do errors in state prediction negate the benefits of search? 3. Continual Learning & Catastrophic Forgetting: Does CLEAR replay at ratio $r=0.5$ successfully preserve historical task competence as world distributions shift across generations, maintaining JEPA representation variance ($\sigma^2 > 10^{-4}$) across all 64 latent embedding dimensions?

    Research Hypotheses & Target Metrics

    • Hypothesis 2.1 (Planning Advantage): Model-based rollouts at horizon $H=3$ achieve $\ge 15\%$ higher fresh task solve rate and lower step count than reflexive drive selection.
    • Hypothesis 2.2 (Rehearsal Stability): CLEAR replay ratio $r=0.5$ prevents policy collapse, maintaining policy entropy $H(\pi) \in [0.8, 2.5]$ and JEPA active dimension health $\ge 60/64$ after 100 GRPO updates.

    Frontier 3: Live Real-Time Dashboard Server (evolzen serve)

    Motivation & Operational Architecture

    While evolzen inspect --export-html dashboard.html provides rich post-hoc inspection of CVTArchive niche centroids and agent demographics, long-running simulations (hours or days) require real-time observability: 1. Low-Overhead Streaming Architecture: A dedicated evolzen serve command serving an asynchronous, event-driven web UI over localhost. 2. Server-Sent Events (SSE) / WebSocket Pipeline: Zero-copy tailing of data/transitions.duckdb in WAL mode, streaming live world tick metrics, population demography, Gini inequality curves, and archive centroid discoveries directly to the browser. 3. Interactive Visual Diagnostics: - Live 2D/3D projection of CVTArchive centroid occupancy (UMAP / PCA / parallel coordinates). - Real-time genealogical tree rendering showing active lineages and extinct branches. - Resource distribution heatmaps across grid locations. - Alert notifications for invariant violations, extinction warnings, or sandbox containment boundaries.

    4. Safety Constraints, Scientific Integrity & Risk Mitigation

    In strict adherence to AGENTS.md and repository safety standards:

    1. MicroVM Sandboxing Boundary (AGENTS.md Rule #1):
    2. Any agent self-modification, dynamic DSL synthesis, or capability generation tested during exploration must never execute outside evolzen.containment.GVisorSandbox.
    3. On hosts where runsc is unavailable (e.g. Darwin macOS development laptops), exploration tests must be strictly guarded with pytest.importorskip or degraded mock verification; live code execution without gVisor is strictly prohibited.
    4. Empirical Disclosure & Anti-Overclaiming (AGENTS.md Rules #4 & #5):
    5. All claims regarding generalization, emergent norm stability, or intelligence must cite specific empirical metrics measured on physical runs or clearly carry the [UNTESTED] designation.
    6. Long-horizon claims (50–100 generations) remain hypothetical until measured via Tier 2 and Tier 3 campaigns.
    7. Data Provenance & Real Data Floor:
    8. Cultural manual storage must strictly enforce real_data_floor \ge 0.2 to prevent self-referential model collapse. Synthetic capabilities generated by agents must be tagged with ProvenanceCategory.SYNTHETIC_DERIVED and verified against exact-match ground truth.
    9. Dependency & Lockfile Integrity (AGENTS.md Rule #2):
    10. All deployments utilize the single unified lockfile (uv.lock). GPU packages (vllm, deepspeed, bitsandbytes, flash-attn, faiss-gpu) are gated exclusively under --extra gpu to maintain a pristine, lightweight local CPU profile.
