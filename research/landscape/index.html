    Comparative Landscape (reviewed 2026-10-21)

    docs/research/literature-review.md gives the six-thread summary mapped to subsystems. This document is the fuller picture behind it — the source-status notes, dated benchmark context, and reasoning that shaped the major design decisions in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, kept in one place so claims do not have to be re-derived per subsystem.

    The field's own yardstick

    ARC-AGI-3 is relevant because it operationalizes one important question: how efficiently can a system learn an unfamiliar interactive task under a fixed action/observation protocol. It is not a universal definition of general intelligence and its leaderboard is date-sensitive. The official ARC material distinguishes public progress from blind evaluation; third-party leaderboard summaries should not be used as permanent baselines. EvolZen records the benchmark date and source when it cites ARC-AGI-3, and uses its own EAE metric for a custom world unless the official protocol is followed exactly [arcprize2026].

    The public-set/private-set gap — the single most load-bearing fact in this design

    A different, important pattern remains: specialized agent harnesses and persistent manuals can substantially improve performance on a reused public set. That is an exposure-regime result, not automatically evidence of cold-start generalization. Vendor or third-party reports are retained as case studies, labeled as such, and are not treated as independent replication [agno2026manual].

    Read this as a methodological warning: reused-set performance and fresh-set performance answer different questions. The exact gap is not a permanent constant and must be re-measured for each system, exposure protocol, model, and benchmark release.

    This is why evolzen.evaluation.procedural_env_gen exists at all, and why it's a hard requirement rather than a nice-to-have: evolzen.evaluation's held-out testing must be designed so it can't quietly collapse into measuring the first thing while believing it measures the second. See docs/phases/2.4-2.5-evaluation.md and evolzen.evaluation.report.EvaluationReport, which makes the reused-vs-fresh gap a required field, not optional.

    Programmatic world-model synthesis

    Several recent approaches use actor/synthesizer/verifier systems that build an executable world model from interaction and check it against replay:

    • OPINE-World (Courtis, Li & Sanner, opineworld2026, arXiv:2607.01531): pairs a qualitative object-oriented natural-language proto-ontology with an executable Python world model and Bayesian uncertainty exploration.
    • Tycho (Lehmann, Aioanei & Vahdati, tycho2026, arXiv:2607.28287): formalizes tasks as parameterized deterministic transition systems and uses active abstraction to decide when to construct, test, repair, or bypass executable hypotheses.
    • Executable World Models (Rodionov, executableworldmodels2026, arXiv:2605.05138): maintains replay-verified Python world models refactored under a Minimum Description Length simplicity bias before action planning.

    These are relevant design precedents, not proof that the method transfers automatically to a persistent social sandbox. EvolZen may use an existing coding-agent harness as a candidate synthesizer, but all generated code remains behind containment and transfer is tested explicitly.

    Representation learning and library learning

    • LeJEPA (Balestriero & LeCun, lejepa2025, arXiv:2511.08544) and When Does LeJEPA Learn a World Model? (lejepaworldmodel2026, arXiv:2605.26379) replace ad-hoc stop-gradient or teacher-student heuristics with Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization (SIGReg), building on I-JEPA (assran2023ijepa, CVPR 2023).
    • DreamCoder (dreamcoder2021, PLDI 2021) and Wake-Sleep Event Semantics (Balogh, wakesleepschank2026, arXiv:2603.25975) demonstrate wake-sleep operator compression over state transformations, while FAISS (johnson2019faiss, IEEE TBDATA 2019) supports sparse vector retrieval over evolzen.library.

    The proposed EvolZen combination is not assumed to be necessary, novel, or effective: it must be compared against simpler state encoders, symbolic baselines, and manual-only conditions.

    Continual learning remains an open engineering risk

    Continual learning remains an empirical risk rather than a solved property (IEEE TPAMI survey, wang2024continualsurvey). CLEAR (rolnick2019clear, NeurIPS 2019) motivates mixing off-policy replay with on-policy updates; TFGN (Ganguli, tfgn2026, arXiv:2605.15053) explores task-free replay-free continual pretraining; and CLaaS (Fallah et al., claas2026, arXiv:2606.05559) investigates online continual adaptation services. Reported numbers are not imported as universal baselines for EvolZen; the predictor phase must measure forgetting, spurious forgetting, forward/backward transfer, adaptation latency, and update cost directly.

    Recent agent-evolution and economic precedents

    • DGM (dgm2026, ICLR 2026), Hyperagents (Zhang et al., hyperagents2026, arXiv:2603.19461), and CORAL (Qu et al., coral2026, arXiv:2604.01658) demonstrate archive-gated empirical self-modification, metacognitive meta-agent editing (DGM-H), and persistent-memory asynchronous multi-agent evolution.
    • Economy of Minds (Qi et al., economyofminds2026, arXiv:2606.02859) and The AI Economist (zheng2022aieconomist, Science Advances 2022) motivate market auctions, wealth feedback, and specialization rules without centralized scripting.

    They do not establish that small local LLM agents with drive vectors will evolve useful social types or grow a persistent world; EvolZen treats those transfers as hypotheses requiring matched ablations.

    Diversity collapse is a testable population risk

    Chen et al. (diversitycollapse2026, ACL 2026 Findings, arXiv:2604.18005) identify how structural coupling, dense communication topologies, and authority-driven hierarchy create "semantic black holes" that collapse idea diversity in multi-agent LLM populations. EvolZen treats those mechanisms as testable hypotheses alongside CVT-MAP-Elites (vassiliades2018cvtmape, IEEE TEVC 2018) and limited-resource QD (qdlimited2024, ICML 2024).

    Containment has moved past cgroup/eBPF

    Systems and agent-security literature — Firecracker KVM microVMs (agache2020firecracker, USENIX NSDI 2020), Safety Must Precede Open-Ended AI (Sheth et al., safetyopenended2025, arXiv:2502.04512), and Beyond Static Sandboxing (Sidik & Rokach, sidikrokach2026, arXiv:2604.11839, documenting ~15× tool overprovisioning in default runtimes and ~73% tool reduction via PPO least-privilege capability scoping) — motivates hardware isolation, continuous runtime evaluation, and fail-closed default-deny governance. The selected deployment backend must still pass a target-host threat model before generated code runs outside tests.
