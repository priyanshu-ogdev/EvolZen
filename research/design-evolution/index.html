    Design Evolution — What Changed, Why, and How It Got Better

    Every mechanism that changed across this project's revisions, in one place: what it was, what it became, why it changed, what got better, and where that improvement is documented. This is the record worth keeping for a paper or a grant application — it shows the reasoning survived, not just the final design. Organized by the subsystem each change now lives in.

    Three structural revisions preceded the current baseline:

    • v0 — the original blueprint ("EvolZen" / "Building the Universe, Building the God"): an ambitious, physically-simulated voxel civilization observed by a central "AGI," targeting AGI directly on a 24-36 month timeline.
    • v1 — the PRD rescoping: every mechanism below was traced to its root failure and replaced with a scoped, citable, buildable equivalent.
    • v2 — the SOTA integration pass: every phase was checked against the cited 2026 literature and upgraded where relevant; actor/synthesizer/verifier systems became a design precedent, not a guarantee of transfer.

    This baseline is the integrated result of all three passes, restructured around nine named subsystems instead of the original two-segment framing (see ADR 0003). The October 2026 audit adds a second correction: the target is now a persistent growing world, not merely a growing rule library observed by a central “god” component.

    evolzen.environment

    Was: a real-time, physically-simulated voxel civilization — a custom C++/CUDA engine, a 5-layer per-voxel state API, a Discrete Element Method structural solver, at a population/city scale requiring datacenter-class compute. Became: a persistent, event-sourced, discrete social sandbox with a seeded typed transition contract, snapshots, a growth ledger, and bounded versioned capability packages. Why: estimated compute for even the agent population alone exceeded the stated three-workstation rig (AGX Spark, DGX Spark, A6000) by several orders of magnitude, and — more importantly — physical realism was never actually the requirement. Verifiability was, and a much smaller domain can be just as verifiable. How it got better: buildable on the actual hardware, while the explicit persistence and growth contract makes the revised research goal measurable. “Moore machine” is retained only as a legacy module name; exact replay is not treated as causal identification.

    Phase 1.1 baseline implementation (2026-11-12, env @ 81ee6eb): upgraded from Protocol and NotImplementedError stubs to a working Python/DuckDB baseline: SocialSandboxEngine (17 ActionType handlers, CapabilityPackage verification gates), schemas/world.py (WorldState, WorldSnapshot, GrowthEvent, InvariantReport, BranchRecord, InterventionSpec), TransitionLogStore (schema-versioned JSON DuckDB persistence, SHA-256 hash chain, atomic snapshots/branches), check_invariants, extract_growth_events / summarize_growth, replay_transitions / replay_from_store / branch_from_snapshot, apply_intervention, adapter (independent host-side legality/age re-validation for agents/development/), SocialSandboxAECEnv (PettingZoo AECEnv wrapper), and run_environment_pilot. An open Phase 1.1 hardening backlog remains (ENV-01–ENV-08 replay/intervention/boundedness fixes, partial mechanics such as ContractRecord cancellation and NormRecord enforcement, expanding adapter.legal_candidates beyond 7 of 17 ActionTypes, invariants.py failure-branch and Hypothesis property tests), and long-horizon as well as target-rig hardware pilots remain untested. Citations: OPINE-World (arXiv:2607.01531), Tycho (arXiv:2607.28287), Executable World Models (arXiv:2605.05138), PettingZoo (arXiv:2009.14471), OMNI-EPIC (arXiv:2405.15568, contrastive boundary), Procgen (arXiv:1912.01588).

    evolzen.agents

    Neuroplasticity mechanism. Was: a rank-expanding LoRA "aging ODE" (plasticity(t) = plasticity_0 · e^(-λt), rank 4→16→64 tied to age) with no mechanism for why rank should track age rather than task complexity. Became: a fixed drive-vector evolved via a Quality-Diversity archive, plus Darwin-Gödel-Machine-style validated self-modification of an agent's own policy code. Why: the original was an invented metaphor with no established continual-learning precedent — unfalsifiable, not buildable-and-testable. How it got better: QD and DGM provide relevant empirical design precedents with validation gates; they do not establish that EvolZen's specific population ontology or self-modification loop will work.

    Reproduction / inheritance mechanism. Was: literal genetic crossover of parents' LoRA weight matrices plus Gaussian mutation. Became: an evolved, low-dimensional drive-vector (never literal weight crossover), with weight-inheritance and data-inheritance kept as separate, explicit tracks. Why: independently trained neural networks don't share an aligned parameter space — the "competing conventions" problem in neuroevolution means naive weight crossover typically breaks both parents' behavior rather than blending it. How it got better: the new mechanism avoids the failure mode entirely, and now has a real open-ended-evolution precedent (DGM's growing archive of self-modified variants) instead of being a purely defensive fix.

    Diversity maintenance. Was: implicit and unmonitored — diversity was assumed to emerge from evolution with no tracking mechanism. Became: a tracked diversity metric as an explicit collapse gate, upgraded to a QD/MAP-Elites archive plus three specific, evidence-based design rules (avoid over-aligned clone populations, avoid dense communication topology, avoid authority-driven hierarchy). Why: diversity collapse in multi-agent LLM populations is now a documented, empirically characterized phenomenon with identified causal factors. How it got better: the design rules are specific and falsifiable, not a vague aspiration. Citations: Darwin Gödel Machine (arXiv:2505.22954), Quality-Diversity (arXiv:2406.03731), Diversity Collapse study (ACL 2026 Findings).

    evolzen.economy

    Was: agents write "books" (serialized policy-weight embeddings deposited to a vector database); other agents read them to warm-start training; no floor on how much of any generation's training data must be real, non-agent-generated material. Became: a mandatory provenance floor plus a tracked diversity metric as an explicit collapse warning and generation-halting circuit breaker; further upgraded to falsifiable, revisable natural-language manuals. A vendor-reported cross-model transfer result is retained as a case study, not as a guaranteed EvolZen effect. Why: training purely on prior generations' own output is the literature's own reference setup for demonstrating model collapse. How it got better: adopts provenance tracking and a collapse circuit breaker, while treating manual transfer as a faster, cheaper, more inspectable channel to measure. The mechanism is matched to the project's compute asymmetry but remains untested here. Citations: Shumailov et al. (Nature, 2024), Agno manual-transfer result (2026), Economy of Minds (arXiv:2606.02859).

    evolzen.exploration

    Was: agents independently discover the arch, the water wheel, and the SR latch through unassisted trial-and-error RL within a fixed simulated timeframe — "give it a reward function" standing in for open-ended discovery. Became: an actor (probes) / synthesizer (writes executable hypothesis code) / verifier (exact-match against replay) loop, exploration steered by ontology error; further upgraded to wiring an off-the-shelf coding-agent harness directly to the environment as the synthesizer. Why: no existing RL research demonstrates open-ended discovery of compositional engineering artifacts even at toy scale — this was the single largest unsupported claim in the original document. How it got better: recent papers provide relevant actor/synthesizer/verifier precedents, so an existing coding-agent harness can be evaluated as a candidate synthesizer rather than rebuilt from scratch. Their benchmark scores are source- and task-specific; transfer to this persistent social sandbox remains untested. Citations: OPINE-World, Tycho, Executable World Models for ARC-AGI-3 (arXiv:2605.05138).

    Causal-understanding layer (verification standard, shared with evolzen.containment). Was: a differentiable Structural Causal Model "Causal Gate" with a stated but uncomputed gradient formula (∇_actual = Gate(causal_score) × ∇_raw). Became: exact-match transition reproduction plus held-out combinations and controlled intervention checks, applied wherever a candidate rule or capability package is promoted. Why: real counterfactual inference requires an identifiable causal graph that cannot generically be discovered from raw multi-agent trajectories; the original formula was notation, not a specified, computable procedure. How it got better: the counterfactual-testing intuition survives in an implementable form, using the same falsifiability bar used by relevant programmatic-modeling systems, strengthened for EvolZen with interventions and validity context instead of treating replay as proof of a causal graph.

    evolzen.library

    Was: a "Slow Weights" base model silently overwritten at the end of each simulated century, plus real-time "Fast Weights" — no forgetting safeguard, no measurement of forgetting. Became: a frozen, growing, verified-rule library, spawn-triggered like a Dirichlet process, protected by a replay buffer and periodic held-out regression tests. Why: silent overwriting is exactly the mechanism catastrophic-forgetting research warns against, and the assumption that this wouldn't be a problem was never tested. How it got better: forgetting is now a measured, tracked, actively-mitigated quantity, calibrated against real 2026 frontier-scale evidence instead of an assumed non-issue. Citations: Ferguson (1973) on Dirichlet process mixture models.

    evolzen.predictor

    Prediction-accuracy target. Was: an inverse-RL reward function with an arbitrary, pre-committed ">90% accuracy 10 time-steps into the future" target. Became: policy-gradient (PPO/GRPO) training against a real, verifiable environment signal, with accuracy reported plainly rather than targeted in advance, protected by deployment-experience rehearsal against forgetting. Why: multi-agent systems with learning components are close to chaotic — small model updates change everyone's policy simultaneously, which tends to hurt long-horizon predictability rather than help it. How it got better: honest, un-gamed reporting, layered with a specific, validated continual-learning mitigation (CLaaS-style rehearsal) instead of an assumption that RL "converges toward" ever-more-general understanding. Citations: CLaaS (arXiv:2606.05559), TFGN (arXiv:2605.15053).

    evolzen.containment

    Was: "complete freedom, complete internet access, agents may bypass the sandbox" — presented as a feature of the system. Became: an explicit containment layer (network egress allow-list, hard resource ceilings, full logging, kill switch); further upgraded to hardware-isolated microVM/gVisor sandboxing plus dynamic capability governance. Why: unrestricted access combined with a reward structure that incentivizes boundary-testing is a real operational risk independent of the system's actual capability level — the same structural situation the AI-boxing/containment literature describes, regardless of framing. How it got better: specifies hardware isolation, default-deny policy, and target-host acceptance instead of treating a backend name as proof of security. Behavior-sensitive governance can tighten access but cannot bypass the static policy. Citations: Bostrom (2014), Yudkowsky (2002), CORAL (arXiv:2604.01658), Sidik & Rokach (arXiv:2604.11839), Safety Must Precede the Deployment of Open-Ended AI (arXiv:2502.04512).

    Ethical/alignment framing. Was: a hard-coded "Constitutional Filter" of "mathematically verifiable ethical constraints," with no account of how conflicts between them get resolved. Became: explicit, testable behavioral rules checked against logged behavior, embedded in a containment-first framing. Why: formally verifying open-ended, potentially-conflicting value constraints is an active, unsolved research area — not buildable in a few months.

    evolzen.recognition

    Knowledge integration ("Oracle API"). Was: query a real-world knowledge base and seed the answer into the simulation when agents plateau technologically — the system doesn't discover, it's told. Became: a growing verified-rule library, then a JEPA + wake-sleep recognition model whose generalization is measured via explicit ablation, plus a natural-language manual layer for fast transfer. Why: querying and injecting an answer isn't grounded generalization — it never tests whether the system can generalize on its own. How it got better: the recognition model's contribution is measured (with/without ablation), not assumed, and the manual layer gives a real, measured transfer-efficiency number instead of a hoped-for one.

    The recognition architecture itself. Proposed as an explicit JEPA + wake-sleep hybrid, not declared a contribution in advance. A JEPA-style component learns candidate state representations; a DreamCoder-style component learns typed operators. Both must be compared with simpler baselines and evaluated with the ablation matrix: recognition on/off, manual on/off, JEPA-only, wake-sleep-only, and combined. Citations: LeJEPA (arXiv:2511.08544) and DreamCoder (PLDI 2021) are component precedents; the fusion and its transfer to this world are untested.

    evolzen.evaluation

    Scale-to-generality claim. Was: vectorize to 1,000 parallel city instances and use MAML to extract "universal laws of civilizational development" — generality proven by raw scale of exposure. Became: two separate tracks: persistent-world longitudinal growth and held-out world-seed/mechanic adaptation scored with EvolZen Action Efficiency (EAE), inspired by but not interchangeable with ARC-AGI-3 RHAE. Why: raw exposure does not establish transfer, and the hardware plan does not justify 1,000 full worlds. How it got better: world growth and fresh adaptation are now measured without conflating their claims.

    The benchmark question itself. Was: no operational definition at all — success was self-referential ("the system understands humanity," "the system passes real-world benchmarks at human-expert level"). Became: explicit persistent-world and fresh-world metrics, with ARC-AGI-3 used only as a dated methodological reference when the protocol is comparable. Why: a custom social sandbox cannot inherit another benchmark's score semantics automatically. How it got better: the project now reports what it actually measures and preserves exposure history as a first-class variable. Citations: ARC Prize Foundation; third-party public-set reports are case studies, not universal baselines.

    Project-level (no single subsystem)

    Real-world transfer / "graduation" claim. Was: fine-tune the system's learned weights on real bridges, real code, and real human social conflict, and claim the result demonstrates cross-domain general intelligence and empathetic reasoning. Became: dropped entirely, replaced by an explicit statement (see docs/PRD.md §4, Non-Goals) of what would and would not count as evidence of progress, scoped to a specific, falsifiable claim about generalization efficiency within a defined task family. Why: there is no demonstrated relationship between "empathy" learned from watching synthetic agent simulations and understanding real human suffering, culture, or context — the single largest unjustified leap in the original document, and no development since has changed that. How it got better: the project can now produce a genuine, publishable result — including a negative one — instead of a claim the evidence was never going to support.

    Why the ceiling holds regardless of any upgrade above

    None of the changes above, individually or stacked together, closes two structural gaps this project's own PRD names honestly (docs/PRD.md §4):

    • Generality is bounded by task diversity, not by how well one task is learned. A system that becomes excellent at this project's environment family has learned one narrow competency, however well — even the impressive 2026 manual-transfer result is a data point about transfer within a family of related games, not evidence against this ceiling.
    • A system cannot distill past its teacher without an external reward. Every component in this design — JEPA, wake-sleep, the manuals, the DGM-style self-modification — is bounded by the base models it starts with, plus whatever real, verifiable signal the environment provides. This is exactly why evolzen.environment's insistence on a genuinely checkable outcome, not a merely plausible one, remains the single most important design decision in the entire plan, upgrades or not.
