# Phase 1.4 — Directed Exploration & Verified Hypothesis Formation

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.exploration`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/exploration/`

## Goal

Use an actor/synthesizer/verifier loop to propose typed rules and capability packages for the
persistent world. Exploration is prioritized by a measured explanation gap over agent types,
mechanics, and world-growth categories. An off-the-shelf coding-agent harness may serve as the
synthesizer, but generated code is untrusted and must execute only inside
`evolzen.containment.Sandbox`.

Exact replay is necessary but not sufficient. Promotion requires replay on held-out transition
combinations, at least one controlled intervention, declared resource bounds, and an explicit
validity context.

## What you build

- actor probes and ontology/explanation-gap prioritization;
- typed hypothesis and capability-package schemas;
- sandboxed synthesis and execution;
- replay, held-out-combination, intervention, and resource-bound verification; and
- false-promotion, false-rejection, and exploration-cost measurements.

## Success metric

Verified hypotheses beat a no-exploration-strategy baseline on held-out transitions and controlled
interventions, while false promotions remain below a pre-registered threshold and no hypothesis
can mutate the engine outside the versioned capability boundary.

## Citations

- **Peer-reviewed journals / conferences:** Romera-Paredes et al. (*FunSearch*, *Nature* 2024, DOI:`10.1038/s41586-023-06924-6`) for evaluator-gated LLM evolutionary program search; Pathak et al. (*ICML* 2017, `arXiv:1705.05363`) for self-supervised prediction-error curiosity; Wang et al. (*Voyager*, *TMLR* 2023) for self-verifying code synthesis.
- **Preprints:** OPINE-World (`arXiv:2607.01531`), Tycho (`arXiv:2607.28287`), and *Executable World Models for ARC-AGI-3* (`arXiv:2605.05138`).

Benchmark results in isolated games or math problems do not establish transfer to a persistent
social sandbox; that transfer is an explicit EvolZen experiment and is **untested**.

## Research context & breakthroughs

- **Verifier-gated program search:** FunSearch (*Nature*, 2024) and *Executable World Models* (`arXiv:2605.05138`) showed that pairing LLM code generators with deterministic evaluators discovers novel verifiable programs while avoiding ungrounded hallucinations.
- **Ontology-error & curiosity prioritization:** Pathak et al. (*ICML* 2017) introduced forward-model prediction error as an intrinsic exploration signal, while OPINE-World (`arXiv:2607.01531`) and Tycho (`arXiv:2607.28287`) prioritize probing actions that falsify current programmatic world hypotheses.
