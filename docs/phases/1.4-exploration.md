# Phase 1.4 — Directed Exploration & Verified Hypothesis Formation

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.exploration`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/exploration/`

## Goal

Use an actor/synthesizer/verifier loop to propose typed rules and capability packages for the
persistent world. Exploration is prioritized by a measured explanation gap over agent types,
mechanics, and world-growth categories. An off-the-shelf coding-agent harness may serve as the
synthesizer, but generated code is untrusted and must execute only inside
`evolzen.containment.Sandbox`.

Exact replay is necessary but not sufficient. Promotion requires replay on held-out transition
combinations, at least one controlled intervention, declared resource bounds, and an explicit
validity context.

## What you build

- actor probes and ontology/explanation-gap prioritization;
- typed hypothesis and capability-package schemas;
- sandboxed synthesis and execution;
- replay, held-out-combination, intervention, and resource-bound verification; and
- false-promotion, false-rejection, and exploration-cost measurements.

## Success metric

Verified hypotheses beat a no-exploration-strategy baseline on held-out transitions and controlled
interventions, while false promotions remain below a pre-registered threshold and no hypothesis
can mutate the engine outside the versioned capability boundary.

## Citations

OPINE-World (arXiv:2607.01531), Tycho (arXiv:2607.28287), and *Executable World Models for
ARC-AGI-3* (arXiv:2605.05138) are relevant precedents for interactive programmatic modeling.
Their benchmark results do not establish transfer to a persistent social sandbox; that transfer is
an explicit EvolZen experiment and is **untested**.
