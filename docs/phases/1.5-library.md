# Phase 1.5 — Growing Rule Library

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.library`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/library/`

## Goal

Store verified rules and capability packages as immutable historical entries without erasing rules
that were valid in earlier world contexts. New versions may supersede old entries explicitly when a
world mechanic changes; routing must respect validity context and never silently rewrite history.

## What you build

- immutable rule/capability records with provenance and evidence links;
- validity intervals or context predicates;
- explicit supersession and deprecation links;
- a held-out transition/intervention regression bank; and
- audit records for spawn, route, promotion, supersession, and rejection decisions.

## Success metric

Historical entries remain readable and replayable across at least three rounds of additions; new
entries improve held-out performance; and any supersession is explicit, reversible, and attributable
to a measured change in world context.

## Citations

Ferguson (1973) motivates the “spawn when existing explanations are inadequate” analogy. The
implemented library is not assumed to be a Dirichlet-process model; its routing and supersession
policy are an EvolZen design decision and are **untested** until measured.
