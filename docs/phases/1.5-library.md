# Phase 1.5 — Growing Rule Library

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.library`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/library/`

## Goal

Store verified rules and capability packages as immutable historical entries without erasing rules
that were valid in earlier world contexts. New versions may supersede old entries explicitly when a
world mechanic changes; routing must respect validity context and never silently rewrite history.

## What you build

- immutable rule/capability records with provenance and evidence links;
- validity intervals or context predicates;
- explicit supersession and deprecation links;
- a held-out transition/intervention regression bank; and
- audit records for spawn, route, promotion, supersession, and rejection decisions.

## Success metric

Historical entries remain readable and replayable across at least three rounds of additions; new
entries improve held-out performance; and any supersession is explicit, reversible, and attributable
to a measured change in world context.

## Citations

- **Peer-reviewed journals:** Ferguson (1973, *Annals of Statistics*) motivating the spawn-when-inadequate routing analogy; Wang et al. (2024, *IEEE TPAMI*, DOI:`10.1109/TPAMI.2024.3367329`) surveying continual-learning isolation and memory architectures; Wang et al. (*Voyager*, *TMLR* 2023) demonstrating compositional skill libraries to mitigate forgetting.

The implemented library is not claimed to be a Bayesian Dirichlet-process model; its routing,
supersession, and reversal policies are EvolZen design choices.

## Research context & breakthroughs

- **External skill libraries vs. parameter drift:** *IEEE TPAMI* (Wang et al., 2024) categorizes parameter-isolation and external-memory architectures as the primary defense against catastrophic forgetting, while Voyager (*TMLR*, 2023) demonstrates non-overwriting skill libraries for lifelong embodied agents. EvolZen extends that model with explicit `ValidityContext`, provenance separation, and reversible supersession links (`env-v1` → `env-v2`) required when environment rules legitimately evolve.

## Codebase progress match

- **Implemented subsystem (`src/evolzen/schemas/library.py`, `src/evolzen/library/`, `src/evolzen/config.py`, `configs/library.yaml`):** Full append-only DuckDB persistence (`LibraryStore`), JSONL file-locked `AuditLog` (`SPAWN`, `ROUTE`, `PROMOTE`, `SUPERSEDE`, `REVERT`, `DEPRECATE`, `REJECT`, `REGRESSION_CHECK`), context compatibility predicates (`validity.py`), `RuleLibrary` orchestrator, and fixture-based `RegressionBank`.
- **Verified tests (`tests/library/`):** **77 passing tests** (**98% statement coverage** across `src/evolzen/library/`), including a 3-round zero-forgetting integration scenario (`test_integration.py`).
- **Scope / untested boundary:** Default routing uses `difflib.SequenceMatcher` guarded by `is_applicable` until Phase 2.3 provides learned embeddings; `RegressionBank.check_rule` validates pre-verified serialisable fixtures without executing `RuleEntry.code`. Live replay against `evolzen.exploration.verifier` (Phase 1.4), `evolzen.containment.Sandbox` (Phase 2.2), and `CapabilityPackage` promotion (`CapabilityPackage.evidence_id` ↔ `VerificationEvidence`, Phase 1.1/1.4) remains **untested** until Phases 1.4 and 2.2 are operational.
