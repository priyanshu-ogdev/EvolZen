# Phase 2.1 — Grounded Predictor

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.predictor`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/predictor/`

## Goal

Build a grounded predictor of world transitions, agent actions, and declared latent profile
variables. Start with a supervised/action-prediction baseline; add PPO/GRPO-style planning only
after its incremental value is measurable. Train against replayable outcomes, not another model's
approval, and protect updates with a declared rehearsal budget.

## What you build

- next-state, outcome, and action prediction baselines;
- optional policy-gradient planning with explicit reward decomposition;
- replay/rehearsal and checkpoint rollback;
- forward transfer, backward transfer, forgetting, adaptation latency, and update-cost metrics;
  and
- separate tests for prediction, intervention response, and planning efficiency.

## Success metric

The predictor reports held-out transition and intervention performance plus longitudinal transfer and
forgetting curves. No fixed accuracy target is accepted in advance; the baseline and all update
costs are reported.

## Citations

- **Peer-reviewed journals / conferences:** Wang et al. (2024, *IEEE TPAMI*, DOI:`10.1109/TPAMI.2024.3367329`) for continual-learning theory and replay/regularization taxonomy; Rolnick et al. (*CLEAR*, *NeurIPS* 2019, `arXiv:1811.11682`) for experience replay in continual RL; Kirkpatrick et al. (*EWC*, *PNAS* 2017, DOI:`10.1073/pnas.1611835114`) for parameter-regularization baselines.
- **Preprints:** DeepSeekMath / GRPO (Shao et al., `arXiv:2402.03300`), CLaaS (`arXiv:2606.05559`), and TFGN (`arXiv:2605.15053`).

Their reported metrics are not imported as universal baselines; matched EvolZen measurements are
required and remain **untested**.

## Research context & breakthroughs

- **Replay & continual RL:** CLEAR (*NeurIPS*, 2019) showed that mixing on-policy updates with bounded off-policy behavioral-cloning replay prevents catastrophic forgetting in sequential environments—directly grounding `RehearsalBuffer`. *IEEE TPAMI* (Wang et al., 2024) formalizes measuring forward transfer, backward transfer, and spurious forgetting together.
- **Critic-free policy optimization (GRPO):** DeepSeekMath (`arXiv:2402.03300`) replaced heavy value networks with group-relative normalized outcome rewards—providing a lower-memory candidate RL optimizer for `Planner` once supervised world-transition baselines exist.

## Codebase progress match

- **Rehearsal buffer (`src/evolzen/predictor/rehearsal_buffer.py`):** Minimal in-memory `RehearsalBuffer` storing/sampling `Transition` records (**2 tests** in `tests/predictor/test_rehearsal_buffer.py`).
- **Planner (`src/evolzen/predictor/planner.py`):** `PredictionResult` dataclass and `Planner` stubs (`predict`, `train_step`) raising `NotImplementedError`; no supervised baseline, GRPO/TRL loop, or transfer/forgetting instrumentation implemented yet.
