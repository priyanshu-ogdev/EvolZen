# Phase 1.2 — The Agents

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.agents`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/agents/`

**Detailed design:** [developmental agents](../subsystems/agents/README.md).
**Implemented increment:** `agents/development/` and `schemas/development.py` contain a
numerical lifecycle/learning/teaching/archive baseline. LLM training and full population/world
integration remain pending.

## Goal

Populate the persistent world with 10–30 active cognitive agents plus optional deterministic
background population processes. Each active agent has an explicit behavioral/social profile:
needs, drives, skills, roles, affiliations, memory scope, communication channels, norms,
capabilities, and lineage. “All types of people” is operationalized as coverage of this ontology,
not as a claim to reproduce real human demographics or psychology.

Drive vectors are evolved on the vector itself, never by literal crossover of independently trained
neural weights. Quality-Diversity selection preserves declared behavioral and social descriptors.
Self-modification is optional in the first baseline and, when enabled, is sandboxed and accepted
only after held-out improvement plus invariant checks.

## What you build

- exactly eight birth-randomized motivational coordinates: pride, greed, lust, envy, gluttony,
  wrath, sloth, Null; a safe bounded equation family rather than generated source programs;
- child/adolescent/adult/elder/dead stages on a world clock, with competence measured separately;
- personal outcome learning and living-parent advice, without copying mastery at birth;
- bounded numeric temperament inheritance and validation-gated behavioral archive admission;

- an `AgentProfile` schema and an explicit descriptor map;
- population and lineage snapshots;
- QD archive coverage, occupancy, persistence, extinction, replacement, specialization, and
  communication metrics;
- a sparse communication/trade graph and non-authoritative role structure; and
- a hardware pilot that records model memory, token throughput, action latency, and scheduling
  overhead on the actual rig.

## Success metric

First satisfy [developmental primitive gates](../subsystems/agents/evaluation.md): reproducible
equations, action/feedback adaptation, life boundaries, parent teaching, and archive safety.
Then measure cultural transfer, age-versus-experience and eight-axis controls on held-out
worlds/families. Passing unit tests does not satisfy the population success metric below.

A 10–30-agent population runs stably for at least five generations, maintains measurable coverage
across the declared descriptor map, and produces at least two persistent role/specialization
patterns without collapsing to one strategy. Any self-modification improvement is reported
separately from population diversity.

## Research context & breakthroughs

- **Intrinsic motivation & developmental stages:** *IEEE TEVC* (Oudeyer et al., 2007) and *IEEE TCDS* (Santucci et al., 2016) formalize competence-progress and autonomous goal discovery across developmental lifetimes—highlighting that EvolZen's current greedy candidate ranking (`decide`) is a numerical baseline requiring a controlled exploration comparison.
- **Quality-Diversity scaling:** MAP-Elites and CVT-MAP-Elites (*IEEE TEVC*, 2018) replace single-objective fitness selection with behavioral repertoire maintenance, directly countering LLM multi-agent homogenization (*ACL 2026 Findings* `diversitycollapse2026`).
- **Self-modification & metacognitive risk:** DGM (*ICLR* 2026) and Hyperagents (`arXiv:2603.19461`) show empirical archive-gated self-editing and emergent persistent memory structures, underscoring why self-modification in EvolZen is gated by `DevelopmentArchive` and `evolzen.containment.Sandbox`.

## Codebase progress match

- **Implemented numerical core (`src/evolzen/agents/development/`, `src/evolzen/schemas/development.py`):** Deterministic eight-coordinate `BirthGenome`, `drive_step`, `born`/`stage_at`/`advance`, `decide`, EMA `observe`/`teach`, and `DevelopmentArchive` are implemented and covered by **32 passing test cases** (29 parametrized cases in `tests/agents/development/` + 3 in `tests/schemas/test_development.py`).
- **Implemented Phase 1.1 environment host bridge (`src/evolzen/environment/adapter.py`, `engine.py`, `pettingzoo_env.py`):** `SocialSandboxEngine`, `WorldState`/`ActionType` (`schemas/world.py`), `SocialSandboxAECEnv`, and `adapter` (`observe_features`, `legal_candidates`, `action_from_candidate`, `execute_decision`) are now implemented (`adapter.legal_candidates` currently surfaces 7 of 17 `ActionType`s).
- **Legacy / stub path (`src/evolzen/agents/drive_vector.py`, `inference.py`, `qd_archive.py`, `self_modification.py`, `schemas/drive_vector.py`):** Five-coordinate `Agent` / `DriveVector`, in-memory `QualityDiversityArchive` (2 tests in `tests/agents/test_qd_archive.py`), `evaluate_proposal` helper, and `TransformersBackend`/`VLLMBackend` raising `NotImplementedError`.
- **Open alignment & unimplemented items:**
  - **5-vs-8 drive reconciliation:** Explicitly reconcile or adapt the legacy 5-axis `DriveVector` path (`agents/{drive_vector,inference,qd_archive,self_modification}.py`) with the 8-axis `BirthGenome`/`Motive` developmental core (`agents/development/`, `environment/adapter.py`).
  - **`AgentProfile` & structured LLM action grammar:** Add the `AgentProfile` schema (roles, affiliations, memory scope, communication channels, norms) linked to `WorldAgentState`, and export `outlines`-compatible `ActionType` + `params` schemas from `schemas/world.py`/`transition.py` to unblock `TransformersBackend`/`VLLMBackend`.
  - **Population orchestration & empirical gates:** Population scheduler loop wiring `agents/development/` (and LLM/QD agents) through `SocialSandboxEngine` / `SocialSandboxAECEnv` + `adapter`, CVT-MAP-Elites scaling, intrinsic-motivation baselines, Ray orchestration, and multi-generation / hardware-pilot empirical runs remain unimplemented and **untested**.
