# Phase 1.2 — The Agents

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.agents`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/agents/`

**Detailed design:** [developmental agents](../subsystems/agents/README.md).
**Implemented increment:** `agents/development/` and `schemas/development.py` contain a
numerical lifecycle/learning/teaching/archive baseline. LLM training and full population/world
integration remain pending.

## Goal

Populate the persistent world with 10–30 active cognitive agents plus optional deterministic
background population processes. Each active agent has an explicit behavioral/social profile:
needs, drives, skills, roles, affiliations, memory scope, communication channels, norms,
capabilities, and lineage. “All types of people” is operationalized as coverage of this ontology,
not as a claim to reproduce real human demographics or psychology.

Drive vectors are evolved on the vector itself, never by literal crossover of independently trained
neural weights. Quality-Diversity selection preserves declared behavioral and social descriptors.
Self-modification is optional in the first baseline and, when enabled, is sandboxed and accepted
only after held-out improvement plus invariant checks.

## What you build

- exactly eight birth-randomized motivational coordinates: pride, greed, lust, envy, gluttony,
  wrath, sloth, Null; a safe bounded equation family rather than generated source programs;
- child/adolescent/adult/elder/dead stages on a world clock, with competence measured separately;
- personal outcome learning and living-parent advice, without copying mastery at birth;
- bounded numeric temperament inheritance and validation-gated behavioral archive admission;

- an `AgentProfile` schema and an explicit descriptor map;
- population and lineage snapshots;
- QD archive coverage, occupancy, persistence, extinction, replacement, specialization, and
  communication metrics;
- a sparse communication/trade graph and non-authoritative role structure; and
- a hardware pilot that records model memory, token throughput, action latency, and scheduling
  overhead on the actual rig.

## Success metric

First satisfy [developmental primitive gates](../subsystems/agents/evaluation.md): reproducible
equations, action/feedback adaptation, life boundaries, parent teaching, and archive safety.
Then measure cultural transfer, age-versus-experience and eight-axis controls on held-out
worlds/families. Passing unit tests does not satisfy the population success metric below.

A 10–30-agent population runs stably for at least five generations, maintains measurable coverage
across the declared descriptor map, and produces at least two persistent role/specialization
patterns without collapsing to one strategy. Any self-modification improvement is reported
separately from population diversity.

## Base Reasoning Model Selection & Sizing Derivation

### 1. Finalized Primary Base Model: `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-AWQ`
Following extensive analysis of reasoning models under 8 billion parameters quantized in 4-bit AWQ (Activation-aware Weight Quantization; Lin et al., *MLSys* 2024, `arXiv:2306.00978`), **`Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-AWQ`** is finalized as the primary base reasoning model across the `evolzen.agents` subsystem.

- **Parameter Count:** 7.61B parameters (strictly $<8\text{B}$).
- **Quantization:** Official Alibaba Cloud 4-bit AWQ (`W4A16`), executed via Marlin INT4 CUDA kernels in `vLLM`.
- **Context Window:** 128k native context (budgeted to 2,048 tokens per agent during live population simulation).
- **License:** Apache 2.0 (commercial & research permissive).
- **Structured JSON Decoding Compatibility:** Direct token-level grammar alignment with `outlines.generate.json` / vLLM `guided_json` mapping directly onto `evolzen.schemas.transition.Action`. Unlike reasoning models that force chain-of-thought `<think>...</think>` prefixes, Qwen2.5-Instruct generates compliant schema objects without parsing failures, infinite loops, or stuttering.

### 3. Mathematical Memory Budget & Serving Derivation

The VRAM requirement for serving an active population of $N$ agents consists of static model weights $M_{\text{weights}}$ and dynamic PagedAttention KV cache $M_{\text{KV}}$:

$$M_{\text{total}} = M_{\text{weights}} + N \times M_{\text{KV\_per\_agent}} + M_{\text{cuda\_overhead}}$$

1. **Model Weights ($M_{\text{weights}}$):**
   - 7.61 billion parameters stored at 4 bits per weight, plus FP16 quantization scales and zero-points (effective $\approx 4.5\text{ bits/param}$):
     $$M_{\text{weights}} \approx \frac{7.61 \times 10^9 \times 4.5 \text{ bits}}{8 \times 1024^3 \text{ bytes/GiB}} \approx 3.99 \text{ GiB} \approx 4.30 \text{ GB}$$
   - Accounting for activation buffers and Marlin kernel scratch space, static weight footprint is **$\approx 4.50\text{ GB}$**.

2. **KV Cache per Agent ($M_{\text{KV\_per\_agent}}$):**
   - Architecture: $L = 28$ transformer layers, Grouped-Query Attention with $N_{\text{kv\_heads}} = 4$ KV heads, head dimension $D_{\text{head}} = 128$, context window $T = 2,048$ tokens, FP16 precision ($P = 2$ bytes):
     $$M_{\text{KV\_per\_agent}} = 2 \times L \times N_{\text{kv\_heads}} \times D_{\text{head}} \times P \times T$$
     $$M_{\text{KV\_per\_agent}} = 2 \times 28 \times 4 \times 128 \times 2 \times 2,048 = 117,440,512 \text{ bytes} \approx 112.0 \text{ MiB} \approx 0.1174 \text{ GB}$$

3. **Population Sizing Derivation:**
   - **10 Agents:** $4.50\text{ GB} + 10 \times 0.1174\text{ GB} \approx 5.67\text{ GB}$ VRAM.
   - **20 Agents:** $4.50\text{ GB} + 20 \times 0.1174\text{ GB} \approx 6.85\text{ GB}$ VRAM.
   - **30 Agents (Phase 1.2 Maximum):** $4.50\text{ GB} + 30 \times 0.1174\text{ GB} \approx 8.02\text{ GB}$ VRAM.

### 5. Tiered Multi-Model Topology

- **Tier-1 (Tactical Action Selection):** `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-AWQ` handles synchronous turn-by-turn action proposals constrained by `outlines.generate.json(Action)` within 256 generated tokens.
- **Tier-2 (Deliberative Rule & Program Synthesis — Phase 1.4):** When an agent enters reflective planning or rule invention, candidate generation is routed to `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B` (or `Qwen/Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct-AWQ`) to leverage deep chain-of-thought mathematical reasoning and code synthesis.
- **Tier-0 (Development / Edge Fallback):** For CPU-only environments or ultra-constrained devices, `Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct-AWQ` or deterministic rule-based heuristic generation (`offline_fallback=True`) guarantees continuous execution.

## 4. Vision-Language (VL) & High-Reasoning Frontier Audit (2025/2026)

### 4.1 Vision-Language vs. Pure-Text Reasoning for 3D Spatial Context & Memory

In a 1×1 km spatial substrate composed of 10,378 chunks, 340 million voxels, 345 wired dwellings, and 64 spatial citizens, **pure-text reasoning models suffer an intrinsic spatial representation bottleneck**:

1. **Text Token Inflation & Coordinate Hallucination:** Serializing 3D coordinates, terrain elevations, and spatial occlusion into text strings (`"Agent at (482, 12, 510), nearest hospital at (450, 20, 550)..."`) consumes 600–1,200 tokens per prompt and forces the LLM to perform arithmetic coordinate subtractions without Euclidean spatial priors, leading to spatial disorientation and navigation hallucination.
2. **Native Spatial Grounding via Multimodal Rotary Position Embeddings (mRoPE):** Advanced Vision-Language Models (e.g., **`Qwen2.5-VL-7B`**, Qwen Team, *arXiv:2502.13923*, Feb 2025) decompose positional rotary embeddings into 3D components $(\Delta t, \Delta y, \Delta x)$. This allows the model to process 2D spatial minimaps, voxel slices, and camera projections natively, grounding agent decisions directly in physical world coordinates.
3. **Episodic Visual-Spatial Memory:** By retaining compact visual-spatial snapshots in `TransitionLogStore` (e.g., dwelling facades, farm plot crop growth stages, and civic beacons), a Vision-Language agent indexes and retrieves spatial memories without context bloating.
4. **Direct Synergy with Grounded JEPA (Phase 2.3):** The non-contrastive Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (`GroundedJEPAEncoder`) operates directly in latent representation space $z \in \mathbb{R}^{32}$. The Vision Transformer (ViT) backbone of a VLM provides structured visual patch embeddings that naturally align with JEPA's target prediction objectives ($L_{JEPA} = \|z_{t+1} - \hat{z}_{pred}\|^2$).

### 4.3 Research Paper Learning Curves & Theoretical Foundations

1. **DeepSeek-R1 Reasoning & Distillation Dynamics (*Guo et al., arXiv:2501.12948*, Jan 2025):**
   - **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization):** Eliminates the critic network via group-normalized relative advantage estimation:
     $$\\mathcal{J}_{GRPO}(\\theta) = \\mathbb{E}\\left[ \\frac{1}{G} \\sum_{i=1}^G \\min\\left( \\frac{\\pi_\\theta(o_i|q)}{\\pi_{old}(o_i|q)} \\hat{A}_i, \\text{clip}\\left(\\frac{\\pi_\\theta(o_i|q)}{\\pi_{old}(o_i|q)}, 1-\\epsilon, 1+\\epsilon\\right) \\hat{A}_i \\right) - \\beta D_{KL}(\\pi_\\theta \\| \\pi_{ref}) \\right]$$
   - **The "Aha! Moment" Phase Transition:** Pure reinforcement learning without supervised warm-up exhibits a distinct inflection point: output token length expands dynamically as the model discovers self-verification, backtracking, and long-horizon reflection.
   - **Distillation Superiority over Small-Model RL:** DeepSeek's empirical findings prove that **distilling R1's reasoning traces into dense 7B/14B student models via Supervised Fine-Tuning dramatically outperforms applying RL directly to small models**, which suffer from reward hacking, language mixing, and instability.
2. **Qwen2.5-VL / 3.5-VL Dynamic Resolution & Spatial Grounding (*Qwen Team, arXiv:2502.13923*, Feb 2025):**
   - Native Dynamic Resolution ViT eliminates artificial downsampling or cropping by representing images as variable patch sequences: $N_{tokens} = (H \\times W) / (28 \\times 28)$.
   - Multi-task coordinate grounding loss curves ($\\mathcal{L} = \\mathcal{L}_{text} + \\lambda_{coord}\\mathcal{L}_{coord} + \\lambda_{bbox}\\mathcal{L}_{bbox}$) converge rapidly, enabling sub-pixel bounding box and spatial coordinate generation.
3. **Spatial Voxel Grounding (*Qwen-3D, OpenReview 2025*; *SpatialVLM, Chen et al., 2024*):**
   - Unprojects visual features into 3D voxel coordinate grids, applying spatial voxel-pooling to compress millions of empty voxels into compact non-empty occupancy tokens.
   - Embeds 3D spatial priors directly into the transformer backbone, preventing coordinate hallucination and grounding spatial agent navigation in metric reality.

### 4.4 Precision Trade-Off Formulation: 8-Bit AWQ vs. 4-Bit AWQ vs. 16-Bit bfloat16

- **8-Bit Activation-Aware Weight Quantization (`W8A16` AWQ):**
  - **Ideal Balance for Live Population Serving:** Quantizes weights to 8 bits ($2^8 = 256$ bins) with group size $g=128$, yielding an effective bitwidth of $8.25\\text{ bits/param}$ ($1.031\\text{ bytes/param}$).
  - Static weight footprint is **$\\approx 9.18\\text{ GB}$**, allowing a 10–20 agent population ($11.12–12.46\\text{ GB}$ VRAM) to fit inside a single 12GB–16GB consumer GPU (RTX 4070 / RTX 4080 Laptop / RTX 4060 Ti 16GB).
  - Recovers **$\\ge 99.8\%$ of FP16 accuracy**, completely eliminating the clipping distortion on visual patch embedding outliers and sub-pixel coordinate boundary errors that occur in 4-bit AWQ.
  - Executes directly on hardware INT8 Tensor Cores (DP4A/GEMM) without per-token dequantization overhead.
- **4-Bit Activation-Aware Weight Quantization (`W4A16` AWQ):**
  - Cuts static weight footprint to $\\approx 4.72\\text{ GB}$ ($4.25\\text{ bits/param}$), enabling extreme co-location on memory-constrained 8GB devices.
  - Suffers $2.8–4.5\%$ coordinate jitter and slight visual patch outlier clipping.
- **Native 16-Bit Precision (`bfloat16`):**
  - Required for **Offline Private Adapter Training**: Preserves exact gradients ($\\nabla W$) and prevents underflow during multi-epoch behavioral cloning and CLEAR replay updates.
  - Sizing: $\\approx 17.80\\text{ GB}$ static weight footprint, requiring 24GB+ GPUs (RTX 3090/4090, A6000).

### 4.5 Tier-2 Deliberation via DoRA (Weight-Decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation)

Rather than maintaining a separate text-only model (e.g. DeepSeek-R1) for Tier-2 deliberative reasoning, EvolZen adopts a **single unified base model (`Qwen 3.5 9B VL W8A16`) with multi-tier adapters**:
1. **The DoRA Formulation (*Liu et al., ICML 2024*, `arXiv:2402.09353`):**
   Standard LoRA couples magnitude and direction into a single low-rank product $BA$. DoRA decomposes weight updates into a learnable magnitude vector $m \\in \\mathbb{R}^{1 \\times k}$ and a normalized directional matrix $V$:
   $$W = m \\odot \\frac{V}{\\|V\\|_c} = m \\odot \\frac{W_0 + \\frac{\\alpha}{r}BA}{\\|W_0 + \\frac{\\alpha}{r}BA\\|_c}$$
   where $m$ is initialized as $\\|W_0\\|_c$. By decoupling magnitude from direction, DoRA closely mirrors full fine-tuning dynamics, outperforming LoRA by 2–4% on mathematical reasoning and program synthesis.
2. **QDoRA over 8-Bit AWQ Base:**
   The $8.9\\text{B}$ parameter base model remains permanently frozen in 8-bit AWQ format in VRAM. Only directional matrices ($A, B$ at rank $r=16, \\alpha=32$) and magnitude vector $m$ are trained in BF16/FP16, producing an ultra-compact adapter checkpoint of **$\\approx 86.5\\text{ MB}$**.
3. **Zero Model-Swapping Overhead:**
   Switching between Tier-1 (tactical action selection) and Tier-2 (deliberative planning & rule synthesis) requires zero PCIe weight transfers: an in-memory adapter switch takes **$< 5\\text{ ms}$**, compared to $1.8–3.5\\text{ seconds}$ for reloading a separate model.
4. **Preservation of 3D Spatial Context in Tier-2:**
   Because Tier-2 uses the same Vision-Language backbone, reflective planning and rule synthesis have direct access to 2D minimaps, voxel slices, and visual episodic memories without coordinate text serialization.
5. **Multi-LoRA Concurrent Batching:**
   Under vLLM continuous batching (`--enable-lora`), Tier-1 action requests and Tier-2 deliberative synthesis requests can be processed concurrently within the **exact same GPU forward pass**.

## Research context & breakthroughs

- **Intrinsic motivation & developmental stages:** *IEEE TEVC* (Oudeyer et al., 2007) and *IEEE TCDS* (Santucci et al., 2016) formalize competence-progress and autonomous goal discovery across developmental lifetimes—highlighting that EvolZen's current greedy candidate ranking (`decide`) is a numerical baseline requiring a controlled exploration comparison.
- **Quality-Diversity scaling:** MAP-Elites and CVT-MAP-Elites (*IEEE TEVC*, 2018; Pugh et al., 2016; Wang et al., 2024) replace single-objective fitness selection with behavioral repertoire maintenance, directly countering LLM multi-agent homogenization (*ACL 2026 Findings* `diversitycollapse2026`).
- **Self-modification & metacognitive risk:** DGM (*ICLR* 2026) and Hyperagents (`arXiv:2603.19461`) show empirical archive-gated self-editing and emergent persistent memory structures, underscoring why self-modification in EvolZen is gated by `DevelopmentArchive` and `evolzen.containment.Sandbox`.
