# Phase 1.3 — Economy & Knowledge Layer

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.economy`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/economy/`

## Goal

Add resource flows, contracts, markets, institutions, norms, and knowledge transfer to the
persistent world. The layer must make specialization and cooperation possible without making
shared manuals an unexamined, recursively self-training channel.

The “real-data floor” is a provenance requirement, not a claim that a synthetic world has real
human data. Sources are labeled as human-authored/external material, human demonstrations,
independently generated simulator traces, or model-generated material. The floor and its sampling
budget are configuration and report fields.

## What you build

- a domain-scoped currency/resource ledger and market mechanism;
- contracts, institutions, and norm proposals with explicit adoption/rejection events;
- falsifiable, revisable manuals with evidence, provenance, scope, and revision history;
- forward/backward knowledge-transfer measurements; and
- world-health checks for resource conservation, concentration, collapse, and recovery from shocks.

## Success metric

At least one durable institution or norm and two distinct specialization patterns emerge across
five generations; manuals are revised after induced falsifications; and the configured provenance
floor remains satisfied without a collapse in population or world diversity.

## Citations

- **Peer-reviewed journals:** Shumailov et al. (2024, *Nature*, DOI:`10.1038/s41586-024-07566-y`) demonstrating recursive model collapse on synthetic generations; Zheng et al. (*The AI Economist*, *Science Advances* 2022, DOI:`10.1126/sciadv.abk2607`) demonstrating two-level multi-agent economic equilibrium and emergent specialization.
- **Preprints / vendor reports:** *Economy of Minds* (`arXiv:2606.02859`, preprint) and vendor manual-transfer reports (`agno2026manual`).

Vendor-reported manual-transfer results are treated as narrow exposure-regime case studies;
replication and economic emergence in EvolZen are **untested**.

## Research context & breakthroughs

- **Model collapse & provenance floors:** Shumailov et al. (*Nature*, 2024) proved that recursively training models on prior synthetic outputs erodes distribution tails and diversity—directly motivating `ManualStore`'s hard provenance separation and `EconomySettings.real_data_floor`.
- **Emergent economic mechanisms:** *The AI Economist* (*Science Advances*, 2022) demonstrated that coupled agent learning and economic policy design yield interpretable specialization and inequality/health trade-offs, motivating `evolzen.economy`'s resource-concentration and shock-recovery metrics.

## Codebase progress match

- **Schemas (`src/evolzen/schemas/manual.py`, `world.py`):** `ManualEntryStatus`, `ManualEntry`, `Manual`, `ProvenanceSource`, and `ProvenanceRecord` are fully implemented, tracking source origin, SHA-256 digests, revision lineages, and evidence links.
- **Implemented Phase 1.3 Economy baseline (`src/evolzen/economy/`):**
  - **Market & Order Book (`market.py`):** Integer currency balances (`NonNegInt`) aligned with `WorldAgentState.currency` and `InstitutionRecord.currency_reserve`; Hayekian dynamic scarcity pricing (`quote_price`, citing Qi et al. 2026); continuous double auction `OrderBook` matching bids/asks at crossing midpoints with institutional fee withholding; typed `ActionType.TRADE` action construction (`create_trade_action`); and direct state settlement (`settle_in_world`) maintaining exact conservation.
  - **Manuals & Provenance Floor (`manuals.py`):** Falsifiable natural-language manuals with empirical falsification (`falsify`), revision (`revise`), and confirmation (`confirm`) chains; automated non-model provenance fraction computation (`calculate_real_fraction`) and generation corpus gating (`verify_generation_corpus`, citing Shumailov et al. 2024); forward knowledge transfer gain measurement (`measure_knowledge_transfer`); and backward compatibility degradation testing (`verify_backward_compatibility`).
  - **Economic Health & Specialization Metrics (`metrics.py`):** Gini coefficient of wealth inequality (`compute_gini_coefficient`, citing Zheng et al. 2022); Herfindahl-Hirschman Index and Shannon entropy for division of labor (`compute_specialization_index`); shock recovery and resilience ratio computation (`compute_shock_recovery`); and macroscopic health evaluation (`evaluate_economic_health`).
  - **Test coverage (`tests/economy/`):** **63 passing tests** across `test_provenance.py`, `test_manuals.py`, `test_market.py`, and `test_metrics.py` (100% statement coverage of `evolzen.economy`).
- **Downstream integration & empirical gates:**
  - Direct integration into developmental teaching passes (`evolzen.agents.development.teach_manual`, `Agent.teach_manual`, and `Lesson.manual_entry`) implemented and verified with 10 unit tests in `tests/agents/test_manual_teaching.py`.
  - Multi-generation empirical runs across five generations measuring emergent specialization and durable institutions on DGX/AGX hardware remain **untested**; success metric remains **unmeasured**.
