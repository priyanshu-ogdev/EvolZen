# Phase 2.4-2.5 — Held-Out Generalization Testing & Evaluation/Publication

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.evaluation`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/evaluation/`

## Goal

Evaluate two distinct outcomes:

1. **Persistent-world growth:** whether the world produces durable artifacts, capabilities,
   institutions, norms, markets, roles, and lineages while preserving replayability and world
   health over long runs.
2. **Fresh-world adaptation:** whether the learning system adapts efficiently to held-out world
   seeds and held-out mechanic combinations with no accumulated history.

Use **EvolZen Action Efficiency (EAE)** for the second track. EAE is inspired by the rationale of
RHAE but must not be called an ARC-AGI-3 score unless the official task and scoring protocol are
followed exactly. Any human/frontier reference must receive the same observations, actions, and
time budget.

## What you build

- persistent-world runs with seed-stratified longitudinal reports;
- fresh-world generation with held-out seeds and mechanic combinations;
- reused-versus-fresh exposure controls;
- EAE scoring, adaptation latency, cost, and failure accounting;
- diversity, growth, health, extinction, and recovery reports; and
- a structured report in which every number traces to a pre-registered metric.

## Success metric

Both tracks produce reproducible results across seeds, all ablations are reported, and the final
report separates world growth from fresh adaptation rather than collapsing them into one headline.

## Citations

- **Peer-reviewed conference:** Cobbe et al. (*Procgen*, *ICML* 2020, DOI:`10.48550/arXiv.1912.01588`) demonstrating why procedurally generated held-out seeds and level distributions are necessary to measure genuine generalization rather than memorization.
- **Preprints & benchmark specs:** Chollet (*On the Measure of Intelligence*, `arXiv:1911.01547`), Chollet et al. (*ARC-AGI-2*, `arXiv:2505.11831`), ARC Prize Foundation ARC-AGI-3 (`arcprize2026`), and Agno vendor report (`agno2026manual`, exposure-regime case study).

The EAE protocol and two-track persistent-vs-fresh evaluation remain **untested** until measured.

## Research context & breakthroughs

- **Procedural OOD splits & public-vs-fresh gap:** Procgen (*ICML* 2020) showed agents trained on fixed levels fail sharply on procedurally generated held-out distributions, while Chollet's *On the Measure of Intelligence* and *ARC-AGI-2* (`arXiv:2505.11831`) formalize measuring skill-acquisition efficiency on novel rule compositions rather than memorized benchmarks. This directly anchors EvolZen's mandatory `reused-vs-fresh` reporting split.
