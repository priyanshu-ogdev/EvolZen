# Phase 1.1 — Environment Engine

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.environment`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/environment/`

## Goal

Build the first persistent, event-sourced, discrete social sandbox for EvolZen. The world must
support agents, locations/resources, artifacts/capabilities, contracts/markets, institutions,
norms/beliefs/manuals, and exogenous events. Its normative transition contract is:

```text
(state_t, action_t, exogenous_input_t, seed) -> (emission_t, state_{t+1})
```

For a fixed complete input record, transitions are deterministic and replayable. The Phase 0
module name `moore_machine.py` is retained for compatibility, but the project uses the more precise
term **seeded, typed interactive transition system**. Exact replay is a mechanistic verification
property, not proof that a causal graph or human intent has been identified.

The first world is deliberately smaller than the original voxel proposal: a persistent graph/grid
of locations, resources, agents, contracts, institutions, norms, and typed capability packages.
Negotiation, markets, and code-with-tests are mechanisms or evaluation slices inside this world,
not alternative definitions of the world.

## What you build

- versioned `WorldSnapshot` records and append-only event logs;
- explicit exogenous-input records and seeded randomness;
- a growth ledger attributing each durable change to an endogenous process, agent action, or
  declared exogenous input;
- snapshot, branch, restore, and replay operations;
- world invariants for conservation, bounded resources, valid ownership, and action legality; and
- a minimal intervention interface for changing one declared variable or withholding one action.

## Success metric

Replay reproduces every emission and next state from the complete logged input. A long run produces
durable growth in at least three allowed categories (for example artifacts, institutions, and
roles), survives a declared environmental shock, and does not require evaluator-injected milestone
events. Hidden mutable state is a failure.

## Citations

### Programmatic World Models & Verifiable State Transitions (2025–2026 SOTA)

- **Tycho** (`arXiv:2607.28287`, `tycho2026`, Lehmann, Aioanei & Vahdati, 2026): active abstraction and deterministic replay verification for discrete interactive transition systems formulated as parameterized Moore machines $\mathcal{M}_\theta^\ell = (S, A, s_0^\ell, \delta_\theta, q_\theta, \varepsilon_\theta)$.
- **OPINE-World** (`arXiv:2607.01531`, `opineworld2026`, Courtis, Li & Sanner, Univ of Toronto, 2026): programmatic world modeling with ontology-error-prioritized interactive exploration over typed, object-centric entity representations.
- **Executable World Models** (`arXiv:2605.05138`, `executableworldmodels2026`, Rodionov, 2026): executable code world-model representations evaluated by exact replay against recorded transitions and verified with minimal description length (MDL) refactoring.

### Formal Transition Systems, Discrete-Event Simulation & Verification (Peer-Reviewed)

- **MCMAS Verification** (`lomuscio2017mcmas`, Lomuscio, Qu & Raimondi, Springer STTT 2017, `10.1007/s10009-015-0378-x`): formal model checking and invariant verification over discrete multi-agent state transition systems.
- **Environment as First-Class Abstraction** (`weyns2007environment`, Weyns, Omicini & Odell, Springer JAAMAS 2007, `10.1007/s10458-006-0012-0`): establishes the environment as the authoritative programmatic arbiter of perception, action legality, and physical/conservation laws.
- **DEVS Discrete Event Formalism** (`zeigler2000simulation`, Zeigler, Praehofer & Kim, Academic Press / Elsevier 2000): Discrete Event System Specification formal contract for discrete state transitions $\delta$ and output emissions $\lambda = e_t$.
- **Trustworthy Provenance & Audit Trails** (`li2022trustworthy`, Li et al., ACM Computing Surveys 2022, `10.1145/3555803`): cryptographic provenance, audit trails, and reproducible simulation logs for verifiable agent claims.

### Multi-Agent Interaction, Sequential Social Dilemmas & Social Sandboxes

- **Concordia** (`arXiv:2312.03664`, `vezhnevets2023concordia`, Vezhnevets et al., Google DeepMind, 2023): generative agent-based modeling grounded by Game Master spatial and social mediation, establishing the precedent for typed social sandboxes.
- **Melting Pot 2.0** (`arXiv:2211.13746`, `agapiou2023meltingpot`, Agapiou et al., NeurIPS 2023): multi-agent social dilemma evaluation suite measuring cooperation, free-riding, and resource sustainability across mixed populations.
- **Sequential Social Dilemmas** (`arXiv:1702.03037`, `leibo2017ssd`, Leibo, Zambaldi, Lanctot, Marecki & Graepel, AAMAS 2017): temporally and spatially extended Markov games with non-linear resource renewal dynamics, moving beyond static normal-form matrix games.
- **The AI Economist** (`arXiv:2108.02755`, `zheng2022aieconomist`, Zheng, Trott, Srinivasa, Parkes & Socher, Science Advances 2022, `10.1126/sciadv.abk2607`): two-level reinforcement learning in dynamic economic sandboxes with taxation, property rights, and public goods.
- **Stratego Multi-Agent Equilibrium** (`perolat2022stratego`, Pérolat et al., Science 2022, `10.1126/science.add4679`): deep Nash equilibrium convergence under imperfect information and private state.
- **Diplomacy & Contractual Reasoning** (`brown2022diplomacy`, Meta FAIR Diplomacy Team, Science 2022, `10.1126/science.ade9064`): natural language communication coordinated with formal game transition engines and binding contracts.
- **PettingZoo** (`arXiv:2009.14471`, `pettingzoo2021`, Terry et al., NeurIPS 2021): standard Agent Environment Cycle (AEC) multi-agent interaction API wrapped by `SocialSandboxAECEnv`.

### Procedural Stratification & Contrastive Safety Boundaries

- **Procgen** (`arXiv:1912.01588`, `procgen2020`, Cobbe et al., ICML 2020): seed-stratified procedural generation measuring generalisation across held-out seeds rather than environment memorization.
- **OMNI-EPIC** (`arXiv:2405.15568`, `omniepic2024`, Faldor, Zhang, Cully & Clune, ICLR 2025): **contrastive boundary** — unlike OMNI-EPIC's unconstrained foundation-model code generation of new game environments, EvolZen explicitly **rejects** unconstrained engine mutation in favor of bounded, versioned `CapabilityPackage` records gated by replay, intervention, and containment checks.

### Legacy Foundations (1984–2010s)

- **Sugarscape** (`epstein1996sugarscape`, Epstein & Axtell, Brookings / MIT Press, 1996): foundational agent-based computational economics baseline (resource gathering, metabolic consumption, reproduction, and bilateral trading rules).
- **The Evolution of Cooperation** (`axelrod1984evolution`, Axelrod, Basic Books, 1984): classical iterated game-theoretic cooperation, reciprocity, and norm emergence in multi-agent populations.
- **Causal Intervention & Structural Invariants** (`pearl2009causality`, Pearl, Cambridge University Press, 2009): formal $\text{do}(X=x)$ counterfactual intervention semantics backing `evolzen.environment.interventions` and counterfactual branch restoration.

The persistent social-sandbox contract, `SocialSandboxAECEnv` integration, and transfer of these precedents to EvolZen's multi-agent setting are EvolZen design choices and remain **untested** empirically until Phase 1.1 long-horizon and target-rig experiments are run.

## Codebase progress match & hardening backlog

- **Implemented Phase 1.1 baseline (`src/evolzen/environment/`, `src/evolzen/schemas/world.py`, `src/evolzen/storage/transition_log.py`):**
  - `SocialSandboxEngine` (`engine.py`) implements `Environment` and `PersistentEnvironment` over `(state_t, action_t, exogenous_input_t, seed) -> (emission_t, state_{t+1})` with zero hidden mutable state on `self`, deterministic RNG via `derive_rng` (`world_state.py`), deterministic `uuid5` `transition_id`/timestamps, and all **17 `ActionType` handlers** (`wait`, `gather`, `move`, `consume`, `craft`, `trade`, `propose_contract`, `fulfill_contract`, `found_institution`, `join_institution`, `contribute_institution`, `propose_norm`, `adopt_norm`, `assign_role`, `spawn_offspring`, `improve_location`, `register_capability`) gated by `CapabilityPackage` verification flags and `world.adulthood_age`.
  - Closed frozen schemas (`extra="forbid"`) in `schemas/world.py` cover all six entity families (`LocationNode`, `WorldAgentState`, `ArtifactRecord`, `CapabilityPackage`, `ContractRecord`, `InstitutionRecord`, `NormRecord`), `ResourceAccounting`, `WorldState`, `WorldSnapshot`, `GrowthEvent`, `WorldInvariant`, `InvariantReport`, `BranchRecord`, and `InterventionSpec`; `Transition` (`schemas/transition.py`) includes `ExogenousInput` and `seed`.
  - `check_invariants` (`invariants.py`), `extract_growth_events` / `summarize_growth` (`growth.py`) across all five `GrowthCategory` buckets, `TransitionLogStore` (`storage/transition_log.py`, `SCHEMA_VERSION = 1` DuckDB JSON-only persistence with SHA-256 hash chaining and atomic `save_snapshot_and_branch`), `replay_transitions` / `replay_from_store` / `branch_from_snapshot` (`replay.py`), `apply_intervention` (`interventions.py`), `adapter` (`observe_features`, `legal_candidates`, `action_from_candidate`, `execute_decision`), `SocialSandboxAECEnv` (`pettingzoo_env.py`), and `run_environment_pilot` (`pilot.py`) are implemented.
- **Landed Stage 1 critical fixes (`src/evolzen/environment/{replay,interventions,engine,invariants}.py`):**
  - `replay_transitions` passes `current_state` (not `recorded.state`) to `engine.step` so trajectory state divergence cascades and is detected.
  - `replay_from_store` queries `to_tick=up_to_tick` and admits transitions with `state.tick <= up_to_tick`, preserving `same_tick=True` multi-agent transitions at `up_to_tick`.
  - `apply_intervention` supports sequence (`list`/`tuple`) indexing in `_set_nested_path` and reconciles `ResourceAccounting.initial_currency` on financial state edits.
  - `_handle_spawn_offspring` and `_check_bounded_state` count active living agents (`sum(1 for a in world.agents.values() if a.alive)`) against `world.max_agents`, eliminating the lineage extinction trap while retaining deceased agent records for immutable lineage auditability.
- **Landed Stage 2 host adapter full 17-action coverage (`src/evolzen/environment/adapter.py`):**
  - `adapter.legal_candidates` and `adapter.action_from_candidate` cover all 17 `ActionType`s (`wait`, `gather`, `move`, `consume`, `craft`, `trade`, `propose_contract`, `fulfill_contract`, `found_institution`, `join_institution`, `contribute_institution`, `propose_norm`, `adopt_norm`, `assign_role`, `spawn_offspring`, `improve_location`, `register_capability`), with dynamic candidate generation and validation in `execute_decision`.
  - Added `cancel_contract:{cid}` candidate generation and translation for proposers of open contracts.
- **Landed Stage 3 operational mechanics (`src/evolzen/environment/{engine,invariants}.py`, `src/evolzen/schemas/world.py`):**
  - **Tool Durability & Fatigue Mitigation:** Handlers `_handle_gather` and `_handle_improve_location` consume tool durability on work actions while halving physical fatigue accumulation.
  - **Norm Compliance & Social Friction:** `_handle_gather` checks conservation norms; common-pool depletion below 20% capacity triggers hypocrisy penalties (-0.1 status/social reward) for adopters and community friction (-0.05 status/social reward) for co-located non-adopters, while sustainable harvesting yields compliance bonuses (+0.02).
  - **Institutional Trade Fee Withholding & Reserves:** `_handle_trade` withholds institution `contribution_rate` fees directly into `local_inst.currency_reserve` while maintaining exact currency conservation; `_handle_contribute_institution` supports direct currency contributions.
  - **Contract Cancellation & Expiration Sweep:** Handlers support voluntary cancellation (`cancel=True` in `_handle_fulfill_contract`) and automatic expiration sweep (`_expire_contracts`) for stale open contracts exceeding TTL (100 ticks) or with deceased parties.
  - **FR-2 Bounded Collections & Status:** Enforced upper bounds on `WorldAgentState.status` (`[0.0, 1.0]`) across schemas, invariants, and engine handlers. Enforced capacity bounds (`artifacts <= 512`, `contracts <= 256`, `institutions <= 64`, `norms <= 64`, `capabilities <= 64`) in `invariants.py` and action rejection.
  - **Exogenous Provenance:** `_apply_exogenous` records unhandled exogenous event types (`unhandled:<kind>`) in `exogenous_applied` transition emissions.
- **Landed Stage 4 PettingZoo AECEnv upgrades & endogenous process attribution (`src/evolzen/environment/{pettingzoo_env,engine}.py`):**
  - **PettingZoo Macro-Shocks & Seeding:** `SocialSandboxAECEnv.step()` accepts `exogenous_input` and deterministic `seed` kwargs, allowing RL and AEC callers to simulate macroeconomic shocks and seed schedules.
  - **TransitionLogStore Hook:** `SocialSandboxAECEnv.__init__` accepts optional `store: TransitionLogStore | None = None` and automatically appends every executed transition with DuckDB hash-chain linkage; surfaces `env.last_transition` and `env.store`.
  - **Expanded 16-Action Discrete Space:** Expanded `_act_space` to `Discrete(16)` with full typed translation and action masks for trade, institution joining, contributions, norm proposals, norm adoptions, and reproduction alongside physical foraging/crafting.
  - **Endogenous Process Growth Attribution:** Added `_expand_endogenous_ecology` in `engine.py`, passively expanding carrying capacity by 1 for undisturbed flourishing ecosystems (`shock_factor == 1.0`, full resource capacity, `next_tick % 50 == 0`), activating the previously dormant `GrowthAttribution.ENDOGENOUS_PROCESS` emitter in `growth.py`.
- **Open Phase 1.1 production-hardening backlog (empirical success metrics remain unmeasured):**
  - **Untested verification & pilot gates:** All 18 failure branches in `invariants.py`, Hypothesis property-based tests for engine/invariants/replay, and long-horizon multi-generation and target-rig hardware pilots remain **untested**; Phase 1.1 empirical success metrics are **not met**.
