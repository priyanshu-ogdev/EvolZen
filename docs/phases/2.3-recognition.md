# Phase 2.3 — Recognition Model & Growing Library

**Subsystem:** `evolzen.recognition`
**Code:** `src/evolzen/recognition/`

## Goal

Test a proposed JEPA-style state representation plus DreamCoder-style wake-sleep operator learning
against simpler baselines in the persistent social sandbox. The combination is a research
hypothesis, not a validated architecture and not evidence of general intelligence.

The rule library is routed sparsely for efficiency, while the manual layer remains a provenance-
tracked fast memory tier. Neither routing confidence nor manual fluency is a verification result.

## What you build

- a state-representation baseline before JEPA;
- JEPA-only, wake-sleep-only, combined, and no-recognition conditions;
- manual-on/manual-off conditions with matched exposure;
- sparse routing with auditable expert selection; and
- persistent-world and fresh-world evaluation cells with confidence intervals and compute/data
  budgets.

## Success metric

The recognition condition improves adaptation latency or success on at least one pre-registered
track without unacceptable regression on the other. All ablation cells, including null or negative
results, are reported.

The proposed combination and its transfer to a persistent social sandbox are **untested**; cited
preprints are hypothesis sources, not validation of EvolZen.

## Research context & breakthroughs

- **Latent predictive encoders (I-JEPA / LeJEPA):** *CVPR* 2023 (`arXiv:2301.08243`) showed predicting masked latent representations avoids brittle pixel/token reconstruction, while `arXiv:2511.08544` simplifies regularization—motivating `JEPAEncoder` strictly as an ablated hypothesis against symbolic/MLP baselines.
- **Wake-sleep operator abstraction + IEEE FAISS routing:** DreamCoder (*PLDI* 2021) alternates task solving (wake) with library refactoring and dreamed replays (sleep), paired here with *IEEE Transactions on Big Data* (Johnson et al., 2019) inner-product indexing (`faiss-cpu` / `faiss-gpu-cu12`) to retrieve candidate `RuleEntry` experts from `evolzen.library.RuleLibrary`.

## Codebase progress match

- **Upstream contracts available:** Phase 1.1 `WorldState`, `TransitionLogStore`, and `adapter.observe_features`, plus Phase 1.5 `RuleLibrary` (`evolzen.library`), are implemented.
- **Routing (`src/evolzen/recognition/embedding_router.py`, `moe_library.py`):** `EmbeddingRouter` implements `sentence-transformers` + `faiss.IndexFlatIP` indexing (**1 optional-dep test** in `tests/recognition/test_embedding_router.py`); `SparseMoELibrary` wires `ExpertRouter` to `evolzen.library.RuleLibrary.get` returning `list[RuleEntry]` (**2 tests** in `tests/recognition/test_moe_library.py`).
- **Grounded JEPA encoder (`src/evolzen/recognition/jepa.py`):** `GroundedJEPAEncoder` implements LeJEPA/I-JEPA self-supervised continuous state representation learning with invariance and variance-regularization objectives (**4 tests** in `tests/recognition/test_jepa.py`).
- **Wake-sleep program abstraction (`src/evolzen/recognition/wake_sleep.py`):** `WakeSleepLearner` implements wake hypothesis proposal (reusing verified rules or synthesizing candidates) and sleep MDL compression refactoring fused with JEPA (**3 tests** in `tests/recognition/test_wake_sleep.py`).
- **Ablation matrix harness (`src/evolzen/recognition/ablations.py`):** `AblationRunner` implements the 4-cell matrix (Baseline, JEPA-only, Wake-Sleep-only, Combined) with 95% confidence intervals and Markdown reporting (**5 tests** in `tests/recognition/test_ablations.py`).
- **Overall status:** Phase 2.3 implementation complete with **15 tests** passing in `tests/recognition/`. Empirical transfer curves remain **untested** until execution on target hardware.
